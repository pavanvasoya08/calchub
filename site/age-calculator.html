About this calculator

This calculator gives your exact age on any date. Many job applications and exams ask for your age on a specific cut-off date, and you can enter that date in the second field.

How it is calculated

It subtracts your date of birth from the chosen date, borrowing days and months as needed. Leap years are handled automatically.

Example

Someone born on 15 May 2000 is 26 years, 6 months and 4 days old on 19 November 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Can I find my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the second date to any date you like.

How is the next birthday calculated?

It counts the days from the chosen date to the next occurrence of your birth day and month.
