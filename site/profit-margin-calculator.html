About this calculator

Profit margin is the share of the selling price that is profit. Markup is the profit as a share of the cost. They are often confused, but they give different numbers.

How it is calculated

Profit = selling price − cost. Margin = profit ÷ selling price × 100. Markup = profit ÷ cost × 100.

Example

If an item costs ₹800 and sells for ₹1,000, the profit is ₹200, the margin is 20% and the markup is 25%.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin is measured against the selling price and markup against the cost. A 25% markup equals a 20% margin.

How do I set a price for a target margin?

Selling price = cost ÷ (1 − target margin). For a 20% margin on a cost of 800, the price is 1,000.
