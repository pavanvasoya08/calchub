About this calculator

Speed, distance and time are linked by one simple relationship. Know any two and you can find the third.

How it is calculated

Speed = distance ÷ time. Distance = speed × time. Time = distance ÷ speed. Keep the units consistent, for example kilometres and hours.

Example

Driving 240 km in 4 hours means an average speed of 60 km/h.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use miles instead of kilometres?

Yes. If you enter miles and hours, the speed will be in miles per hour.

Is this the average speed?

Yes. It does not account for stops or changes in speed.
