About this calculator

This calculator tells you how much to put aside each month to reach a financial goal such as a car, a wedding or a house down payment. It takes your current savings and expected interest into account.

How it is calculated

It finds the monthly deposit P so that your current savings plus all monthly deposits, growing at the monthly rate, equal the goal at the end of the period.

Example

Set the return to 0% if you are saving in cash. Then the monthly amount is simply the remaining goal divided by the number of months.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator include inflation?

No. If your goal's cost will rise with inflation, increase the goal amount to match.

What if I can't afford the monthly amount?

Extend the time period, lower the goal or look for options with a higher return.
