About this calculator

Return on Investment (ROI) measures how much you gained or lost on an investment compared to its cost. It is useful for comparing different investments.

How it is calculated

ROI = (Final value − Amount invested) ÷ Amount invested × 100. Annualized return = (Final value ÷ Amount invested)1 ÷ years − 1.

Example

Buying for ₹1,00,000 and selling for ₹1,35,000 gives a profit of ₹35,000 and an ROI of 35%.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good ROI?

It depends on the risk and time. Compare it against the returns of safer options such as fixed deposits.

Why is annualized return useful?

A 35% gain over 1 year is far better than over 10 years. Annualizing makes investments of different durations comparable.
