About this calculator

This calculator converts hourly pay into weekly, monthly and annual income. It is helpful when comparing a freelance or hourly job with a fixed salary.

How it is calculated

Annual pay = hourly rate × hours per week × weeks per year. Monthly pay = annual pay ÷ 12.

Example

At ₹500 an hour for 40 hours a week and 52 weeks, the annual pay is ₹10,40,000, which is about ₹86,667 a month.

Frequently asked questions

Should I subtract unpaid leave?

Yes. If you take unpaid weeks off, reduce the weeks per year to get a realistic figure.

Is the result before or after tax?

Before tax. Taxes and deductions are not included.
