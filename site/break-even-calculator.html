About this calculator

The break-even point is where your total revenue equals your total costs, so you make neither profit nor loss. Every unit sold beyond it adds to your profit.

How it is calculated

Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ (selling price − variable cost per unit). The difference between price and variable cost is called the contribution margin.

Example

With fixed costs of ₹1,00,000, a price of ₹500 and a variable cost of ₹300, the contribution is ₹200 per unit, so you must sell 500 units.

Frequently asked questions

What are fixed and variable costs?

Fixed costs, such as rent, stay the same regardless of sales. Variable costs, such as materials, change with each unit produced.

How can I lower my break-even point?

Cut fixed costs, reduce variable costs or raise your selling price.
