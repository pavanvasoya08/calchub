HRA Exemption Calculator – Tax-Free House Rent Allowance

Find how much of your House Rent Allowance is tax-exempt under the old tax regime.

About this calculator

If you live in rented accommodation and receive HRA from your employer, part of it can be exempt from income tax under the old regime. The exemption is the lowest of three amounts.

How it is calculated

Exempt HRA = the least of (1) actual HRA received, (2) rent paid minus 10% of (basic + DA), and (3) 50% of (basic + DA) for metro cities or 40% for others.

Example

With a basic of ₹50,000, HRA of ₹20,000, rent of ₹18,000 in a metro city: the three amounts are ₹20,000, ₹13,000 and ₹25,000. The exemption is ₹13,000 a month.

Frequently asked questions

Can I claim HRA if I live with my parents?

Yes, if you actually pay rent to them and have proof, such as rent receipts and bank transfers. Tax rules apply, so check with a tax professional.

Is HRA exemption available in the new tax regime?

HRA exemption is generally available only under the old tax regime. Check the latest rules.
