About this calculator

Standard deviation measures how spread out numbers are around their mean. A small value means the numbers are close together, and a large value means they are widely spread.

How it is calculated

Population SD = √(Σ(x − mean)2 ÷ n). Sample SD = √(Σ(x − mean)2 ÷ (n − 1)). Variance is the square of the standard deviation.

Example

For 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9 the mean is 5 and the population standard deviation is 2.

Frequently asked questions

Which should I use, sample or population?

Use the population SD if your data includes every member of the group. Use the sample SD if the data is only a sample of a larger group.

Why does the sample formula divide by n − 1?

It corrects the bias that comes from estimating the mean from the same data.
