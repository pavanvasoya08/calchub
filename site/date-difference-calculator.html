About this calculator

Use this tool to find how long there is between two dates, for example the days left until a deadline or the length of a project.

How it is calculated

It counts the whole days between the two dates and also breaks the gap into years, months and days. Working days count Monday to Friday and do not include public holidays.

Example

From 1 January 2026 to 31 January 2026 is 30 days, which is 4 weeks and 2 days.

Frequently asked questions

Is the end date included?

The result counts the days between the dates. Add 1 if you want to include both the start and end dates.

Are holidays excluded from working days?

No. Only weekends are excluded. Subtract public holidays yourself.
