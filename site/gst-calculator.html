About this calculator

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is an indirect tax charged on the sale of goods and services. The same method works for VAT or sales tax, just enter your local rate.

How it is calculated

To add tax: tax = amount × rate ÷ 100. To remove tax: net amount = tax-inclusive amount × 100 ÷ (100 + rate), and tax = inclusive amount − net amount.

Example

Adding 18% GST to ₹1,000 gives GST of ₹180 and a total of ₹1,180. Removing 18% GST from ₹1,180 returns ₹1,000.

Frequently asked questions

What are CGST and SGST?

For sales within one state, the GST is split equally between the central government (CGST) and the state government (SGST). Sales between states attract IGST.

Do GST rates change?

Yes, rates and categories are revised from time to time. Always confirm the current rate for your product or service from an official source.
