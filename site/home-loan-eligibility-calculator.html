Loan Eligibility Calculator – How Much Loan Can You Afford?

Estimate the maximum loan you may qualify for based on your income, existing EMIs and the interest rate.

About this calculator

Lenders limit the share of your income that can go towards loan repayments. This calculator works backwards from that limit to estimate the largest loan whose EMI fits within it.

How it is calculated

Maximum EMI = income × allowed % − existing EMIs. Eligible loan = maximum EMI ÷ (EMI per 1 unit of loan at your rate and tenure).

Example

With a net income of ₹1,00,000, existing EMIs of ₹10,000 and a 50% limit, you can afford an EMI of ₹40,000 a month. The loan that EMI supports depends on the rate and tenure you enter.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage of income do banks allow for EMIs?

Many lenders allow roughly 40% to 60% of net monthly income to go towards all EMIs combined. The exact limit depends on the lender and your profile.

Is this the amount the bank will sanction?

No. This is an estimate. Banks also check your credit score, age, employment stability and the value of the property or asset.
