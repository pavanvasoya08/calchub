About this calculator

Ideal body weight formulas give a rough target weight for a given height and sex. They were originally created to help with medicine dosing, and are only a guide.

How it is calculated

Devine: 50 kg + 2.3 kg for each inch over 5 feet for men, 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg for women. Robinson and Miller use slightly different values.

Example

A man who is 170 cm (about 5 ft 7 in) tall has a Devine ideal weight of about 66 kg.

Frequently asked questions

Why do the formulas give different answers?

Each was derived from different data, so they differ by a few kilograms. Treat the results as a range.

Is the healthy BMI range better?

The BMI range gives a wider and often more practical target than a single number.
