About this calculator

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is the number of calories your body burns at rest to keep you alive. Multiplying it by an activity factor gives the calories you need each day to maintain your weight.

How it is calculated

Mifflin-St Jeor: BMR = 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age + 5 for men, or − 161 for women. Daily calories = BMR × activity factor.

Example

A 30-year-old man who weighs 70 kg and is 170 cm tall has a BMR of 1618 kcal a day.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

Eating less than your maintenance calories creates a deficit. Speak to a doctor or dietitian before making big changes.

How accurate is this?

It is an estimate. Real needs vary with muscle mass, genetics and health conditions.
