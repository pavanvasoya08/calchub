Brick Calculator

Estimate how many standard bricks and how much mortar you need for a wall, after deducting doors and windows.

About this calculator

This calculator uses the standard Indian modular brick of 190 x 90 x 90 mm. With a 10 mm mortar joint each brick occupies 200 x 100 x 100 mm, which gives 50 bricks per square metre of half-brick wall.

How it is calculated

Net wall area = length x height - openings. Bricks = net area x 50 (half brick), 100 (one brick) or 150 (one and a half brick), plus wastage. Mortar = wall volume - brick volume, where one brick is 0.001539 m3.

Example

A 5 m x 3 m half-brick wall with 2 m2 of openings has 13 m2 net area, so 650 bricks, or 683 with 5% wastage. Mortar needed is about 0.17 m3.

Frequently asked questions

How many bricks are needed per square metre?

About 50 for a half-brick wall and 100 for a one-brick wall when using 190 x 90 x 90 mm bricks with 10 mm mortar joints.

How much mortar do I need?

Mortar is roughly 15% to 25% of the wall volume. The calculator shows the wet mortar volume.

Do I need to deduct doors and windows?

Yes. Deduct the area of every opening larger than about 0.1 m2 to avoid ordering too many bricks.
