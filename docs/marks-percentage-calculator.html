About this calculator

This calculator adds your marks across subjects and shows your overall percentage, grade and division. Leave maximum marks at 0 for subjects you do not need.

How it is calculated

Percentage = total marks obtained / total maximum marks x 100.

Example

Marks of 78, 85, 92, 67 and 74 out of 100 each total 396 out of 500, which is 79.2% (grade B, first division).

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate percentage from marks?

Divide your total marks by the maximum marks and multiply by 100.

Can subjects have different maximum marks?

Yes. Each subject has its own maximum, and the calculator uses the totals.

What is first division?

Commonly 60% and above, second division 45% to 59%, third division 33% to 44%. Boards differ.
