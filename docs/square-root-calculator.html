About this calculator

The square root of a number is the value that, multiplied by itself, gives that number. The calculator also handles cube roots and nth roots.

How it is calculated

The nth root of x is x raised to the power 1/n. The square root is the case n = 2.

Example

The square root of 50 is about 7.0711 because 7.0711 x 7.0711 is about 50. It lies between the perfect squares 49 (7 x 7) and 64 (8 x 8).

How to use the Square Root Calculator

  1. Fill in Number, Root, Root index (n).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The calculator follows standard mathematical definitions. Very large or very small numbers may be shown rounded.
  • Check that your inputs use the units or format the tool asks for (for example, a percentage entered as 12, not 0.12).

Frequently asked questions

Can I take the square root of a negative number?

Not in real numbers. Odd roots such as the cube root do work for negative numbers.

What is a perfect square?

A whole number that is the square of another whole number, such as 16, 25 and 144.

How do I estimate a square root by hand?

Find the two nearest perfect squares, then refine the guess by averaging and dividing.

Is this square root calculator free to use?

Yes. The square root calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
