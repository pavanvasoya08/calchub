About this calculator

Inflation is the steady rise in prices over time. The same amount of money buys less each year, so it is important to plan savings and retirement with inflation in mind.

How it is calculated

Future cost = amount × (1 + inflation)years. Purchasing power of today's amount in the future = amount ÷ (1 + inflation)years.

Example

At 6% inflation, something that costs ₹1,00,000 today will cost about ₹1,79,085 in 10 years.

Frequently asked questions

What inflation rate should I use?

Look at your country's long-term average. Many people use 5% to 7% for India and 2% to 3% for developed economies.

Why does inflation matter for savings?

If your savings earn less than inflation, your money loses purchasing power even though the balance grows.
