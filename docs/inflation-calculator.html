Find how much the same goods will cost in the future and how much your money will be worth.

About this calculator

Inflation makes prices rise over time, so the same amount of money buys less. This tool shows the future cost of something that costs a fixed amount today, and the buying power of your money.

How it is calculated

Future cost = Amount x (1 + Inflation)^Years. Buying power = Amount / (1 + Inflation)^Years.

Example

Something that costs ₹1,00,000 today will cost about ₹1,79,085 after 10 years at 6% inflation. Your ₹1,00,000 will only buy goods worth ₹55,839 in today's terms.

How to use the Inflation Calculator

  1. Fill in Amount today (₹), Average inflation (% per year), Years.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results use standard formulas and the values you enter. Banks, brokers and tax authorities may apply fees, rounding rules or different compounding, so real figures can differ.
  • Interest rates, tax slabs and investment returns change over time. Market-linked returns (SIP, mutual funds, stocks) are not guaranteed.
  • This tool is for estimation and learning. It is not financial, tax or investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

What inflation rate should I use?

India's long-term average is around 5% to 6% a year. Use a higher rate for education and healthcare costs.

How can I beat inflation?

Investments that earn more than inflation after tax, such as equity funds over the long term, preserve buying power. Savings accounts often earn less than inflation.

Is inflation the same for everything?

No. Food, fuel, rent, education and healthcare all rise at different rates. Use the rate that fits your expense.

Is this inflation calculator free to use?

Yes. The inflation calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
