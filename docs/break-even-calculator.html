Break-Even Calculator

About this calculator

Shows the sales volume where your total revenue equals your total costs, so you neither gain nor lose.

How it is calculated

Contribution per unit = price - variable cost. Break-even units = fixed costs / contribution per unit (rounded up). Break-even revenue = units x price.

Example

With fixed costs of ₹50,000, a price of ₹250 and a variable cost of ₹150, the contribution is ₹100 per unit. You break even at 500 units, or ₹1,25,000 of revenue.

How to use the Break-Even Calculator

  1. Fill in Fixed costs (₹), Selling price per unit (₹), Variable cost per unit (₹), Target profit (₹, optional).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results depend on the costs, prices and fee rates you enter. Marketplace fees, GST and platform rules change often, so confirm current rates with the platform or your accountant.
  • Figures are estimates for planning and are not accounting, tax or legal advice.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a fixed cost?

Costs that stay the same whatever you sell, such as rent, salaries and software subscriptions.

What counts as a variable cost?

Costs that rise with each unit sold, such as materials, packaging and per-order shipping.

How can I lower my break-even point?

Raise the price, cut variable costs or reduce fixed costs. Each one lowers the units you need to sell.

Is this break-even calculator free to use?

Yes. The break-even calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
