About this calculator

Commission is a fee paid to a salesperson, agent or broker, usually as a percentage of the sale value.

How it is calculated

Commission = sale amount × commission rate ÷ 100.

Example

A 5% commission on a ₹2,50,000 sale is ₹12,500.

Frequently asked questions

Is commission calculated before or after tax?

That depends on your agreement. Confirm whether the rate applies to the pre-tax or post-tax amount.

Can I use a tiered commission?

This calculator uses a single rate. For tiers, calculate each slab separately and add the results.
