Commission Calculator

Calculate sales commission at a flat rate or with a higher rate above a sales threshold.

About this calculator

Helps sales teams, agents and marketplaces work out earned commission.

How it is calculated

Flat: commission = sales x rate. Tiered: commission = sales up to the threshold x rate 1 + sales above it x rate 2.

Example

A flat 5% on ₹2,00,000 of sales earns ₹10,000. With a tier at ₹1,00,000 and 8% above it, you would earn ₹13,000.

How to use the Commission Calculator

  1. Fill in Total sales (₹), Commission type, Commission rate (%), Tier threshold (₹), Rate above threshold (%).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results depend on the costs, prices and fee rates you enter. Marketplace fees, GST and platform rules change often, so confirm current rates with the platform or your accountant.
  • Figures are estimates for planning and are not accounting, tax or legal advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the effective commission rate?

It is total commission divided by total sales. It matters most in tiered plans.

Is commission taxable?

Usually yes. It counts as income, and TDS may apply. Check your local tax rules.

Are commissions paid on gross or net sales?

That is set by your agreement. Some plans exclude returns, taxes or discounts, so enter the amount your plan uses.

Is this commission calculator free to use?

Yes. The commission calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
