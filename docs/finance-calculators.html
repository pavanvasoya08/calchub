Frequently asked questions

Are the finance calculators accurate?

They use standard financial formulas and update instantly. Real offers can differ because of fees, taxes and rounding, so treat the results as a close estimate.

Do you store the numbers I enter?

No. All calculations run in your browser and nothing you type is sent to a server.

Which finance calculator should I start with?

Most people start with the EMI calculator for loans, the SIP calculator for investing and the income tax calculator for yearly planning.
