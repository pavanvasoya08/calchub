Word Counter

Paste your text to count words, characters, sentences and paragraphs, and see the reading and speaking time.

About this calculator

Type or paste text into the box and the counts update as you type. It is useful for essays, social posts, meta descriptions and any text with a word limit.

How it is calculated

Words are groups of characters separated by spaces or line breaks. Sentences end with a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark. Paragraphs are separated by blank lines. Reading time uses 200 words per minute and speaking time 130.

Example

The sample text has 33 words and 2 paragraphs, so it takes about 10 seconds to read.

How to use the Word Counter

  1. Fill in Your text.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Real-world speeds, file sizes and compression vary from the theoretical values used here.
  • Text and password tools run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded or stored.

Frequently asked questions

How are words counted?

Any run of characters separated by whitespace counts as one word.

Is my text saved?

No. The counting happens in your browser and the text is never uploaded.

How long does 1,000 words take to read?

About 5 minutes at 200 words per minute.

Is this word counter free to use?

Yes. The word counter is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
