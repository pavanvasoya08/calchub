Paint Calculator

Calculate the wall area of a room, subtract doors and windows and find the litres of paint for the number of coats you want.

About this calculator

Enter the room size and the calculator finds the paintable wall area, deducting an average door (1.9 m2) and window (1.2 m2). Multiply by the number of coats and divide by the coverage per litre to get the paint quantity.

How it is calculated

Wall area = 2 x (L + W) x H - doors x 1.9 - windows x 1.2. Ceiling area (optional) = L x W. Litres = area x coats / coverage. Most emulsions cover 9 to 12 m2 per litre per coat.

Example

A 5 m x 4 m room with 3 m walls, 2 doors and 2 windows has 47.80 m2 of wall. Two coats at 10 m2 per litre need 9.56 litres.

How to use the Paint Calculator

  1. Fill in Room length (m), Room width (m), Room height (m), Number of doors, Number of windows, Number of coats and 2 more option(s).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Quantities are theoretical estimates. Real projects need extra for wastage, site conditions and design changes.
  • Rates for materials, labour, stamp duty and registration vary by city and change often. Confirm with your contractor, engineer or the local registration office.

Frequently asked questions

How much area does one litre of paint cover?

Typically 9 to 12 square metres (100 to 130 sq ft) per coat, depending on the paint and the surface.

How many coats do I need?

Two coats are standard. A rough or dark wall may need a primer and an extra coat.

Should I buy extra?

Add about 5% for touch-ups and uneven absorption.

Is this paint calculator free to use?

Yes. The paint calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
