About this calculator

A ratio compares two quantities. Simplifying it to lowest terms makes it easier to read, and solving a proportion finds a missing quantity.

How it is calculated

To simplify, divide both numbers by their greatest common divisor. To solve A : B = C : x, use x = B x C / A.

Example

24 : 36 simplifies to 2 : 3 because both are divisible by 12. In 2 : 3 = 10 : x, x = 3 x 10 / 2 = 15.

How to use the Ratio Calculator

  1. Fill in Mode, A, B, A, B, C.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The calculator follows standard mathematical definitions. Very large or very small numbers may be shown rounded.
  • Check that your inputs use the units or format the tool asks for (for example, a percentage entered as 12, not 0.12).

Frequently asked questions

What is a ratio?

A way to compare two quantities, written as A : B or A/B.

How do I use ratios in recipes?

Scale every ingredient by the same factor. To double a recipe, multiply each quantity by 2.

Can I use decimals?

Yes. The calculator converts decimals to whole numbers before simplifying.

Is this ratio calculator free to use?

Yes. The ratio calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
