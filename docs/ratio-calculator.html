About this calculator

A ratio compares two quantities. Simplifying it to lowest terms makes it easier to read, and solving a proportion finds a missing quantity.

How it is calculated

To simplify, divide both numbers by their greatest common divisor. To solve A : B = C : x, use x = B x C / A.

Example

24 : 36 simplifies to 2 : 3 because both are divisible by 12. In 2 : 3 = 10 : x, x = 3 x 10 / 2 = 15.

Frequently asked questions

What is a ratio?

A way to compare two quantities, written as A : B or A/B.

How do I use ratios in recipes?

Scale every ingredient by the same factor. To double a recipe, multiply each quantity by 2.

Can I use decimals?

Yes. The calculator converts decimals to whole numbers before simplifying.
