About this calculator

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) lets you invest a fixed amount every month in a mutual fund. Because your earlier investments keep earning returns, long periods benefit greatly from compounding.

How it is calculated

Future value = P × [((1 + i)n − 1) ÷ i] × (1 + i), where P is the monthly amount, i is the monthly rate of return and n is the number of months.

Example

Investing ₹10,000 a month for 10 years at 12% a year grows to about ₹23,23,391, while you invest ₹12,00,000 in total.

Frequently asked questions

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund returns depend on the market. This calculator only shows an estimate based on the rate you enter.

What return rate should I use?

Use a conservative number. Long-term equity funds have historically delivered around 10% to 12% a year in some periods, but actual returns can be much higher or lower.

Is it better to start early?

Yes. Starting early gives your money more time to compound, which often matters more than the amount you invest.
