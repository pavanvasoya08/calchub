Tip Calculator

Calculate a tip and split the total bill between any number of people.

About this calculator

Helps you tip fairly and divide the bill evenly.

How it is calculated

Tip = bill x tip %. Total = bill + tip. Per person = total / number of people.

Example

A ₹2,400 bill with a 10% tip is ₹240, for a total of ₹2,640. Split between 4 people, each pays ₹660.

How to use the Tip Calculator

  1. Fill in Bill amount (₹), Tip (%), Number of people.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Prices, taxes and tariffs differ from shop to shop and state to state. Enter the actual figures from your bill or price tag.
  • Results are estimates for comparing options and are not a substitute for an official bill.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I tip?

In India 5-10% is common at restaurants when no service charge is added. In the USA 15-20% is typical.

Is a service charge the same as a tip?

No. A service charge is added by the restaurant. A tip is optional and goes to staff.

Can I round the result?

Yes. Many people round the total per person up to the next whole number.

Is this tip calculator free to use?

Yes. The tip calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
