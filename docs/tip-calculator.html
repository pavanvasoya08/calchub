About this calculator

Use this calculator at restaurants to add a tip and divide the bill fairly. Tipping customs differ by country, so choose the percentage that suits where you are.

How it is calculated

Tip = bill × tip% ÷ 100. Total = bill + tip. Each person pays total ÷ number of people.

Example

A ₹2,400 bill with a 10% tip is ₹2,640. Split among 4 people, each pays ₹660.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I tip?

It depends on local custom. Many places use 5% to 10%, while some countries expect 15% to 20%.

Is the service charge the same as a tip?

Not always. A service charge is added by the restaurant, while a tip is optional. Check your bill first.
