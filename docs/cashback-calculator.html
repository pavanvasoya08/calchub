About this calculator

Credit cards and wallets often cap cashback. This shows what you actually get.

How it is calculated

Cashback = purchase amount x cashback %, limited to the cap. Effective price = purchase amount - cashback.

Example

A ₹5,000 purchase at 5% cashback would give ₹250, but a ₹200 cap limits it to ₹200. The effective price is ₹4,800.

How to use the Cashback Calculator

  1. Fill in Purchase amount (₹), Cashback (%), Maximum cashback (₹, 0 for no cap).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Prices, taxes and tariffs differ from shop to shop and state to state. Enter the actual figures from your bill or price tag.
  • Results are estimates for comparing options and are not a substitute for an official bill.

Frequently asked questions

Is cashback taxable?

Cashback on purchases is generally treated as a discount, not income. Check local rules for referral rewards.

What if there is no cap?

Enter 0 in the cap field.

Is cashback better than a discount?

A discount reduces what you pay now. Cashback arrives later and may have limits, so compare the effective price.

Is this cashback calculator free to use?

Yes. The cashback calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
