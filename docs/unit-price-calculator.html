About this calculator

Bigger packs are not always cheaper. Divide price by quantity to find the real best value.

How it is calculated

Price per unit = price / quantity. The product with the lower price per unit is the better value.

Example

Product A costs ₹120 for 500 g, which is ₹0.24 per gram. Product B costs ₹220 for 1000 g, which is ₹0.22 per gram, so B is cheaper per gram.

How to use the Unit Price Calculator

  1. Fill in Product A price (₹), Product A quantity, Product B price (₹), Product B quantity.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Prices, taxes and tariffs differ from shop to shop and state to state. Enter the actual figures from your bill or price tag.
  • Results are estimates for comparing options and are not a substitute for an official bill.

Frequently asked questions

Do both quantities need the same unit?

Yes. Use the same unit for both, such as grams for both or millilitres for both.

Is the cheaper unit price always the better buy?

Not if you will not use all of it. Food that spoils or items you do not need are not savings.

Can I compare more than two products?

Yes, use the Price Comparison Calculator to compare up to four.

Is this unit price calculator free to use?

Yes. The unit price calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
