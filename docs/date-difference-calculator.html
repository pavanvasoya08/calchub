About this calculator

This calculator measures the time between two dates in years, months, days, weeks and hours.

How it is calculated

The earlier date is subtracted from the later one. Years and months are counted first, with the remaining days shown last. Tick the option to count both the start and end day.

Example

From 1 January 2026 to 21 November 2026 is 10 months and 20 days, which is 324 days or 46 weeks and 2 days.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator include the end date?

Not by default. Tick the option if you want to count both the first and last day.

What if my start date is after the end date?

The calculator swaps them so you always get a positive difference.

Can I use it for deadlines?

Yes. Enter today as the start date and the deadline as the end date.
