About this calculator

Recipes use cups and spoons, but kitchen scales use grams. Because ingredients have different densities, the conversion depends on what you are measuring.

How it is calculated

Volume is converted using 1 cup = 236.6 ml, 1 tablespoon = 14.8 ml and 1 teaspoon = 4.93 ml. Weight uses the ingredient's density, for example 1 cup of flour is about 125 g.

Example

1 cup of all-purpose flour is about 125 g, 1 cup of sugar about 200 g and 1 cup of water about 237 g. One tablespoon of butter is about 14 g.

How to use the Cooking Measurement Calculator

  1. Fill in Amount, From, To, Ingredient.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Prices, taxes and tariffs differ from shop to shop and state to state. Enter the actual figures from your bill or price tag.
  • Results are estimates for comparing options and are not a substitute for an official bill.

Frequently asked questions

Why does 1 cup of flour weigh less than 1 cup of sugar?

Flour is lighter and more airy. Each ingredient has its own density, so grams per cup differ.

Are cup measures the same everywhere?

No. A US cup is 236.6 ml, a metric cup is 250 ml and an Indian tea cup is often 150-200 ml. This tool uses the US cup.

How accurate are the gram values?

They are averages. Packing flour or sugar differently changes the weight, so use a scale when precision matters.

Is this cooking measurement calculator free to use?

Yes. The cooking measurement calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
