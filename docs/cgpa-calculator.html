About this calculator

Cumulative grade point average (CGPA) combines your results from all semesters into one number. You can use a simple average or weight each semester by its credits.

How it is calculated

Simple CGPA = sum of SGPAs / number of semesters. Weighted CGPA = sum of (SGPA x credits) / total credits. Percentage = CGPA x multiplier (9.5 for CBSE).

Example

For SGPAs of 8.2, 8.5, 7.9 and 8.8 the CGPA is 33.4 / 4 = 8.35. With the 9.5 multiplier this is about 79.3%.

How to use the CGPA Calculator

  1. Fill in SGPA of each semester (separated by commas), Credits of each semester (optional, leave empty for a simple average), Percentage multiplier, Grading scale (maximum grade point).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Grading rules differ between boards, universities and countries. Use your institution's official conversion where one exists.
  • Results are for guidance and do not replace an official marksheet or transcript.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between SGPA and CGPA?

SGPA is the grade point average for one semester. CGPA is the average across all completed semesters.

Which multiplier should I use?

CBSE uses 9.5. Many universities use 10 or their own formula, so check your institution's rules before submitting the result anywhere official.

Should I use credits?

If your university weights semesters by credits, enter them. Otherwise the simple average is fine.

Is this cgpa calculator free to use?

Yes. The cgpa calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
