About this calculator

Use this calculator to add or subtract durations, for example total work time or video length. The result is shown as hours, minutes and seconds, as well as decimal hours.

How it is calculated

Convert each time to seconds (hours x 3600 + minutes x 60 + seconds), add or subtract, then convert back.

Example

2:45:30 + 1:30:45 = 9,930 + 5,445 = 15,375 seconds, which is 4 hours 16 minutes 15 seconds (4.2708 decimal hours).

How to use the Time Calculator

  1. Fill in Time 1: hours, Time 1: minutes, Time 1: seconds, Operation, Time 2: hours, Time 2: minutes and 1 more option(s).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Grading rules differ between boards, universities and countries. Use your institution's official conversion where one exists.
  • Results are for guidance and do not replace an official marksheet or transcript.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add times?

Add seconds first, carry over to minutes every 60, then add minutes and carry over to hours.

Can I get decimal hours?

Yes. The result also shows decimal hours, which is useful for pay calculations.

What if the result is negative?

That means the second time is larger than the first. The result is shown with a minus sign.

Is this time calculator free to use?

Yes. The time calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
