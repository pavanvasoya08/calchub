Discount Calculator

Find the sale price and how much you save after one or two discounts.

About this calculator

Enter the original price and the discount to see the price you pay and how much you save. You can add a second discount, such as a coupon on top of a sale.

How it is calculated

Savings = Price x Discount / 100. Final price = Price - Savings. For a second discount, it is applied on the already reduced price.

Example

A ₹2,000 item with 20% off costs ₹1,600, so you save ₹400. With an extra 10% off, the price falls to ₹1,440.

How to use the Discount Calculator

  1. Fill in Original price (₹), Discount (%), Extra discount (%, optional).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results use standard formulas and the values you enter. Banks, brokers and tax authorities may apply fees, rounding rules or different compounding, so real figures can differ.
  • Interest rates, tax slabs and investment returns change over time. Market-linked returns (SIP, mutual funds, stocks) are not guaranteed.
  • This tool is for estimation and learning. It is not financial, tax or investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a discount?

Multiply the price by the discount percentage and divide by 100. Subtract the result from the price.

Is 20% + 10% the same as 30% off?

No. The second discount is applied on the reduced price, so 20% and 10% together equal 28% off.

Can I find the discount percentage from two prices?

Yes. Discount % = (Original price - Sale price) / Original price x 100.

Is this discount calculator free to use?

Yes. The discount calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
