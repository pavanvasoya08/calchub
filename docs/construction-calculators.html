Frequently asked questions

Are material estimates exact?

No. They follow standard thumb rules and include an allowance for wastage. Ask your contractor to confirm quantities.

Do stamp duty rates differ by state?

Yes. Stamp duty and registration charges vary by state, so the calculator lets you enter your own rates.
