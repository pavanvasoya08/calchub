About this calculator

To convert a percentage back to CGPA, divide it by the same multiplier that your board or university uses.

How it is calculated

CGPA = percentage / multiplier. For CBSE the multiplier is 9.5.

Example

A percentage of 76 divided by 9.5 gives a CGPA of 8.0.

How to use the Percentage to CGPA Calculator

  1. Fill in Percentage (%), Divisor.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Grading rules differ between boards, universities and countries. Use your institution's official conversion where one exists.
  • Results are for guidance and do not replace an official marksheet or transcript.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert percentage to CGPA for CBSE?

Divide the percentage by 9.5.

Which multiplier should I use?

CBSE uses 9.5. Many universities use 10 or their own formula, so check your institution's rules before submitting the result anywhere official.

Can the CGPA be more than 10?

No. If the result exceeds 10, check the percentage and multiplier.

Is this percentage to cgpa calculator free to use?

Yes. The percentage to cgpa calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
