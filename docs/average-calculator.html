About this calculator

The average, or arithmetic mean, shows the central value of a set of numbers. The calculator also gives the median, mode and range.

How it is calculated

Mean = sum of values / count. Median = middle value after sorting (average of the two middle values if the count is even). Mode = most frequent value. Range = largest - smallest.

Example

For 12, 18, 25, 30 and 45 the sum is 130 and the count is 5, so the mean is 26. The median is 25 and the range is 45 - 12 = 33.

How to use the Average Calculator

  1. Fill in Numbers (separated by commas, spaces or new lines).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The calculator follows standard mathematical definitions. Very large or very small numbers may be shown rounded.
  • Check that your inputs use the units or format the tool asks for (for example, a percentage entered as 12, not 0.12).

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between mean and median?

The mean is affected by very large or small values. The median is the middle value and is less sensitive to outliers.

Can there be more than one mode?

Yes. If several values appear equally often, all of them are modes.

How do I find a weighted average?

Multiply each value by its weight, add the results and divide by the total of the weights.
