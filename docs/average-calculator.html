About this calculator

The mean is the usual average. The median is the middle value when numbers are sorted. The mode is the value that appears most often. Together they describe a data set.

How it is calculated

Mean = sum ÷ count. Median = the middle value (or the mean of the two middle values). Range = maximum − minimum.

Example

For 12, 18, 25, 25, 31, 44: the sum is 155, the mean is 25.83, the median is 25, the mode is 25 and the range is 32.

Frequently asked questions

When should I use the median instead of the mean?

Use the median when extreme values would distort the mean, such as income data.

Can a data set have more than one mode?

Yes. If several values tie for the most frequent, all are modes.
