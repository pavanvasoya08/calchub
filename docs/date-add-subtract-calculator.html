About this calculator

Choose a start date, then add or subtract a number of days, weeks, months or years. The new date is shown with its weekday.

How it is calculated

Days and weeks are added as 1 and 7 days each. Months and years keep the same day of the month, and use the last day of the month if it does not exist.

Example

20 November 2026 plus 90 days is 18 February 2027, a Thursday.

How to use the Date Add/Subtract Calculator

  1. Fill in Start date, Operation, Amount, Unit.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Dates use the Gregorian calendar and your device's local time zone unless the tool says otherwise.
  • Public holidays differ by country and state, so holiday-based results only count the holidays you enter.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the date 90 days from today?

Leave the start date as today, choose Add, enter 90 and choose Days.

What happens to 31 January plus 1 month?

It becomes the last day of February.

Can I subtract years?

Yes. Choose Subtract and Years.

Is the weekday shown?

Yes, the weekday of the new date is always displayed.

Is this date add/subtract calculator free to use?

Yes. The date add/subtract calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
