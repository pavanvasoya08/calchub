About this calculator

Choose a start date, then add or subtract a number of days, weeks, months or years. The new date is shown with its weekday.

How it is calculated

Days and weeks are added as 1 and 7 days each. Months and years keep the same day of the month, and use the last day of the month if it does not exist.

Example

20 November 2026 plus 90 days is 18 February 2027, a Thursday.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the date 90 days from today?

Leave the start date as today, choose Add, enter 90 and choose Days.

What happens to 31 January plus 1 month?

It becomes the last day of February.

Can I subtract years?

Yes. Choose Subtract and Years.

Is the weekday shown?

Yes, the weekday of the new date is always displayed.
