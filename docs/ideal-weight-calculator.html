About this calculator

Ideal body weight formulas estimate a healthy weight from your height and sex. This calculator shows four common formulas and their average.

How it is calculated

Each formula adds a fixed amount of weight for every inch of height over 5 feet. For example, Devine: men 50 kg + 2.3 kg per inch, women 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg per inch.

Example

A man who is 170 cm tall (about 66.9 inches) has a Devine ideal weight of 50 + 2.3 x 6.9 = 65.9 kg.

How to use the Ideal Weight Calculator

  1. Fill in Sex, Height (cm).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results are general estimates based on population formulas and do not replace a medical examination.
  • Age, genetics, muscle mass, medical conditions and medication can all change what is healthy for you.
  • This calculator is for information only and is not medical advice. Please consult a doctor or a registered dietitian before making health decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Are ideal weight formulas accurate?

They are rough guides. Frame size, muscle mass and age all matter, so use them together with BMI and waist measurements.

Why do the formulas give different answers?

They were developed from different data sets and for different purposes, so the results differ by a few kilograms.

Is this calculator a substitute for medical advice?

No. It uses standard formulas to give a general estimate. Individual needs vary, so please consult a doctor or registered dietitian for personal advice.

Is this ideal weight calculator free to use?

Yes. The ideal weight calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
