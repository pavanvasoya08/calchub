About this calculator

Body Mass Index (BMI) compares your weight with your height. It is a quick screening tool for weight categories in adults, not a diagnosis.

How it is calculated

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)2. Adult ranges: under 18.5 underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 normal, 25 to 29.9 overweight, 30 and above obese.

Example

A person who weighs 70 kg and is 170 cm tall has a BMI of 24.2, which is in the normal range.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. BMI does not measure body fat or muscle, so it can be misleading for athletes, older adults and some ethnic groups.

Does this apply to children?

No. Children and teens use age and sex-specific BMI percentiles. Ask a paediatrician.
