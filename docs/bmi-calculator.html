About this calculator

Body mass index (BMI) compares your weight with your height. It is widely used to screen for underweight, overweight and obesity in adults.

How it is calculated

BMI = weight in kg / (height in metres)^2. Categories: below 18.5 underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 normal, 25 to 29.9 overweight, 30 and above obese.

Example

A person who weighs 70 kg and is 170 cm tall has a BMI of 70 / (1.70 x 1.70) = 24.2, which is in the normal range.

How to use the BMI Calculator

  1. Fill in Units, Weight (kg), Height (cm), Weight (lb), Height (feet), Height (inches).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results are general estimates based on population formulas and do not replace a medical examination.
  • Age, genetics, muscle mass, medical conditions and medication can all change what is healthy for you.
  • This calculator is for information only and is not medical advice. Please consult a doctor or a registered dietitian before making health decisions.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy BMI?

For most adults a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered healthy.

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

Not always. BMI cannot tell muscle from fat, so muscular people may show a high BMI even with low body fat.

Does BMI work for children?

Children and teenagers are assessed using BMI-for-age percentiles, so this adult calculator should not be used for them.

Is this calculator a substitute for medical advice?

No. It uses standard formulas to give a general estimate. Individual needs vary, so please consult a doctor or registered dietitian for personal advice.

Yes. The bmi calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
