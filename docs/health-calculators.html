Frequently asked questions

Are these health calculators medical advice?

No. They give general estimates from standard formulas. For personal medical advice, please consult a qualified doctor.

Why do BMI and body fat results differ?

BMI only compares weight with height. Body fat estimates use body measurements, so the two can tell different stories, especially for athletic people.

How often should I recalculate?

Every few weeks is enough. Daily changes in weight are mostly water and are not meaningful.
