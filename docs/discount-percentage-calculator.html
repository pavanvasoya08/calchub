Discount Percentage Calculator

Find the discount percentage from the original and sale price, or the sale price from a discount percentage.

About this calculator

Quickly check how big a sale really is, or see what a percentage discount will cost you.

How it is calculated

Discount % = (original price - sale price) / original price x 100. Sale price = original price x (1 - discount % / 100).

Example

A ₹2,000 item on sale for ₹1,500 has a discount of 25% and you save ₹500. A 30% discount on ₹2,000 gives a price of ₹1,400.

How to use the Discount Percentage Calculator

  1. Fill in Original price (₹), I know the, Sale price (₹), Discount (%).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Prices, taxes and tariffs differ from shop to shop and state to state. Enter the actual figures from your bill or price tag.
  • Results are estimates for comparing options and are not a substitute for an official bill.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a discount percentage?

Subtract the sale price from the original price, divide by the original price and multiply by 100.

Is a 50% discount the same as half price?

Yes. Taking 50% off means you pay half of the original price.

Does the discount include tax?

No. Taxes are added after the discount. Use the Final Price Calculator to include tax.

Is this discount percentage calculator free to use?

Yes. The discount percentage calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
