About this calculator

Total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) is the number of calories you burn in a day. Eating this amount keeps your weight steady.

How it is calculated

TDEE = BMR x activity multiplier. Multipliers: 1.2 sedentary, 1.375 light, 1.55 moderate, 1.725 very active, 1.9 extra active.

Example

A 30-year-old man (70 kg, 170 cm) with a BMR of 1618 kcal who exercises moderately has a TDEE of 1618 x 1.55 = 2507 kcal.

How to use the TDEE Calculator

  1. Fill in Sex, Age (years), Weight (kg), Height (cm), Activity level.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results are general estimates based on population formulas and do not replace a medical examination.
  • Age, genetics, muscle mass, medical conditions and medication can all change what is healthy for you.
  • This calculator is for information only and is not medical advice. Please consult a doctor or a registered dietitian before making health decisions.

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose my activity level?

Pick the level that matches your typical week. If unsure, choose the lower one because people often overestimate activity.

How do I lose weight using TDEE?

Eat somewhat below your TDEE. A deficit of 300 to 500 kcal a day is a common, sustainable starting point.

Is this calculator a substitute for medical advice?

No. It uses standard formulas to give a general estimate. Individual needs vary, so please consult a doctor or registered dietitian for personal advice.

Is this tdee calculator free to use?

Yes. The tdee calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
