Rent Calculator

Work out your total rent with yearly increases, or estimate the HRA exemption you can claim on rent paid.

About this calculator

Use this calculator to plan what a rented home will cost you over several years, including a yearly rent increase and maintenance, or to estimate the House Rent Allowance (HRA) exemption available to salaried employees under the old tax regime.

How it is calculated

Rent in year n = rent x (1 + increase %)^(n-1). Total rent = sum of yearly rents x 12. HRA exemption per month = lowest of: HRA received, rent paid - 10% of basic salary, and 50% (metro) or 40% (non-metro) of basic salary.

Example

Rent of ₹20,000 a month with a 5% yearly increase costs ₹13,26,152 over 5 years. For HRA, with basic ₹50,000, HRA ₹20,000 and rent ₹18,000 in a metro, the monthly exemption is ₹13,000.

How to use the Rent Calculator

  1. Fill in What do you want to calculate?, Monthly rent (₹), Monthly maintenance (₹), Security deposit (₹), Yearly rent increase (%), Years and 4 more option(s).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Quantities are theoretical estimates. Real projects need extra for wastage, site conditions and design changes.
  • Rates for materials, labour, stamp duty and registration vary by city and change often. Confirm with your contractor, engineer or the local registration office.

Frequently asked questions

Who can claim HRA exemption?

Salaried employees who pay rent and receive HRA, and who choose the old tax regime. The new regime does not allow HRA exemption.

What is a normal yearly rent increase?

Many agreements use 5% to 10% a year. Your rental agreement sets the actual figure.

Is the security deposit included in total rent?

No. It is refundable, so it is shown separately.

Is this rent calculator free to use?

Yes. The rent calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
