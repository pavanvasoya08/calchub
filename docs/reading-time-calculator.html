Reading Time Calculator

Paste your text or enter a word count to find out how long it takes to read silently and aloud.

About this calculator

Average adults read about 200 to 250 words per minute silently and speak at around 130 words per minute. Enter your own reading speed for a personal estimate.

How it is calculated

Reading time = words / words per minute. Speaking time = words / 130.

Example

A 1,500-word article takes 7.5 minutes to read at 200 words per minute.

How to use the Reading Time Calculator

  1. Fill in Your text (optional), Or enter a word count (0 = use the text), Reading speed (words per minute).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Real-world speeds, file sizes and compression vary from the theoretical values used here.
  • Text and password tools run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded or stored.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average reading speed?

About 200 to 250 words per minute for adults.

How do blogs show reading time?

Most divide the word count by 200 or 238 and round to the nearest minute.

Should I count images?

Not in this calculator. Add a few seconds for each image if you want to be precise.

Is this reading time calculator free to use?

Yes. The reading time calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
