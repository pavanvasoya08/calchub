Carpet Area Calculator

Convert super built-up or built-up area to the usable carpet area of a flat, using typical loading and wall percentages.

About this calculator

Carpet area is the floor space you can actually cover with a carpet, excluding walls, balconies and shared areas. Builders usually quote super built-up area, so this calculator works backwards using the loading percentage and wall share. Both percentages are editable because they differ by project.

How it is calculated

Built-up area = Super built-up / (1 + loading %). Carpet area = Built-up / (1 + wall %). Under RERA, the price must be quoted on carpet area, so always check the carpet area in the agreement.

Example

A flat with 1,200 sq ft super built-up area and 25% loading has a built-up area of 960.00 sq ft. With 15% wall addition the carpet area is 834.78 sq ft, about 69.57% of the super built-up area.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between carpet and super built-up area?

Carpet area is the usable floor inside the walls. Super built-up area adds walls, balconies and a share of common areas such as lifts and lobbies.

What is a normal loading percentage?

In many Indian projects loading is between 20% and 35%. Ask the builder for the exact figure in writing.

Does RERA use carpet area?

Yes. The Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act requires sale agreements to state the carpet area.
