Shipping Cost Calculator

Find volumetric and chargeable weight for a parcel and estimate the shipping cost from slab rates.

About this calculator

Couriers bill on the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight. This tool shows both and applies your rates.

How it is calculated

Volumetric weight (kg) = length x width x height (cm) / 5000. Chargeable weight = the higher of actual and volumetric weight. Cost = first slab rate + extra slabs x additional rate.

Example

A 30 x 20 x 15 cm parcel has a volumetric weight of 1.80 kg. At an actual weight of 1.2 kg the chargeable weight is 1.8 kg, which is 4 slabs of 0.5 kg: ₹165.

How to use the Shipping Cost Calculator

  1. Fill in Length (cm), Width (cm), Height (cm), Actual weight (kg), Volumetric divisor, Weight slab (kg) and 2 more option(s).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results depend on the costs, prices and fee rates you enter. Marketplace fees, GST and platform rules change often, so confirm current rates with the platform or your accountant.
  • Figures are estimates for planning and are not accounting, tax or legal advice.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the divisor 5000?

Many Indian couriers use 5000 for cm. Some use 4000 or 6000, so check with yours and edit the field.

Are these courier rates accurate?

No. The rates are placeholders. Enter the slab rates from your courier or aggregator.

Does it include GST or fuel surcharge?

No. Add those separately based on your courier's rate card.

Is this shipping cost calculator free to use?

Yes. The shipping cost calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
