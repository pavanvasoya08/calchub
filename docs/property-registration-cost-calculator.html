Property Registration Cost Calculator

Estimate the total government cost of registering a property: stamp duty plus registration charges, using indicative state rates you can edit.

About this calculator

Registering a property involves stamp duty and a registration fee, both based on the property value or the circle rate, whichever the state treats as higher. This calculator uses indicative state rates; choose Custom to enter the exact rates your state applies.

How it is calculated

Stamp duty = property value x stamp duty %. Registration fee = property value x registration %. Total = stamp duty + registration fee. Rates here are indicative for a typical residential sale to a male buyer and change often.

Example

For a ₹50,00,000 property in Maharashtra at 5% stamp duty and 1% registration: stamp duty ₹2,50,000, registration ₹50,000, total ₹3,00,000. Maharashtra caps the registration fee at ₹30,000 in many cases, so the final figure may be lower.

How to use the Property Registration Cost Calculator

  1. Fill in Property value (₹), State, Custom stamp duty (%), Custom registration fee (%).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Quantities are theoretical estimates. Real projects need extra for wastage, site conditions and design changes.
  • Rates for materials, labour, stamp duty and registration vary by city and change often. Confirm with your contractor, engineer or the local registration office.

Frequently asked questions

Are these rates exact?

No. They are indicative and states revise stamp duty, surcharges and caps regularly. Always confirm with the sub-registrar office or your state's official portal.

Is duty charged on the sale price or the circle rate?

Most states charge on whichever is higher: the agreement value or the government circle rate.

Do women pay less?

Several states give women buyers a concession on stamp duty. Use the Stamp Duty Calculator to see the difference.

Is this property registration cost calculator free to use?

Yes. The property registration cost calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
