ROI Calculator

Calculate return on investment as a percentage and as an annual growth rate over time.

About this calculator

Compare how well different investments or projects performed.

How it is calculated

ROI = (final value - amount invested) / amount invested x 100. Annualised ROI = (final value / amount invested)^(1 / years) - 1.

Example

Investing ₹1,00,000 and ending with ₹1,50,000 gives a gain of ₹50,000, an ROI of 50% and an annualised ROI of 14.47% over 3 years.

How to use the ROI Calculator

  1. Fill in Amount invested (₹), Final value (₹), Time period (years).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results depend on the costs, prices and fee rates you enter. Marketplace fees, GST and platform rules change often, so confirm current rates with the platform or your accountant.
  • Figures are estimates for planning and are not accounting, tax or legal advice.

Frequently asked questions

Why does annualised ROI matter?

A 50% gain over 3 years is not the same as 50% in one year. Annualising makes investments of different lengths comparable.

Does ROI include costs and fees?

Only if you include them in the amount invested or the final value.

Can ROI be negative?

Yes. If the final value is below the investment, ROI is negative and shows your loss.

Is this roi calculator free to use?

Yes. The roi calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
