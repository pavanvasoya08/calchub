Countdown Calculator

See a live countdown in days, hours, minutes and seconds to any future date and time.

About this calculator

Pick a date and time and this countdown updates every second to show how long is left. It works for birthdays, holidays, exams, launches and deadlines.

How it is calculated

Time left = target date and time minus the current time of your device. The result is split into whole days, hours, minutes and seconds. If the target is in the past, the time since it passed is shown instead.

Example

A countdown to 1 January 2027 at midnight, checked on 20 November 2026 at midnight, shows 42 days, 0 hours, 0 minutes and 0 seconds.

How to use the Countdown Calculator

  1. Fill in Target date and time.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Dates use the Gregorian calendar and your device's local time zone unless the tool says otherwise.
  • Public holidays differ by country and state, so holiday-based results only count the holidays you enter.

Frequently asked questions

Does the countdown use my local time?

Yes. The target is read in your device's time zone.

What happens when the time is over?

The calculator shows how much time has passed since the target.

Do I need to keep the page open?

The numbers update while the page is open. If you reload, it recalculates from the current time.

Does it count days or business days?

It counts calendar days. For business days use the Business Days Calculator.

Is this countdown calculator free to use?

Yes. The countdown calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
