Square Feet Calculator

Enter the length and width of up to four rooms to get the total area in square feet, square metres and square yards.

About this calculator

This calculator multiplies length by width for each room, adds the areas and converts the total to square feet, square metres and square yards (gaj). Leave a room at zero to ignore it.

How it is calculated

Area = length x width. Total area = sum of all rooms. 1 sq ft = 0.092903 sq m, and 1 sq yd = 9 sq ft. Other units are first converted to feet (1 m = 3.28084 ft, 1 in = 1/12 ft, 1 yd = 3 ft).

Example

A 12 ft x 10 ft room and a 10 ft x 10 ft room give 120 + 100 = 220 sq ft, which is 20.44 sq m or 24.44 sq yd.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate square feet?

Multiply the length in feet by the width in feet. For irregular rooms, split the space into rectangles, calculate each and add them.

How do I convert square feet to square metres?

Multiply square feet by 0.092903. For example, 1,000 sq ft is about 92.9 sq m.

Can I enter dimensions in metres or inches?

Yes. Pick the unit and the calculator converts everything to square feet for you.
