Frequently asked questions

How do I compare two products fairly?

Use the unit price or price comparison calculator. It divides each price by the quantity so you can see the cheapest per unit.

Are the bill calculators exact?

Utility tariffs differ by provider, so the calculators use rates you enter. Check your latest bill for the correct values.
