About this calculator

Plan your trip budget by working out how much fuel you will use and what it costs.

How it is calculated

Fuel needed = distance ÷ mileage. Cost = fuel needed × price per litre.

Example

A 300 km trip at 15 km per litre uses 20 litres. At ₹100 a litre, the fuel costs ₹2,000.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my car's mileage?

Fill the tank, note the odometer, drive, then refill. Divide the distance by the litres added.

Does driving style change mileage?

Yes. Speed, traffic, load and AC use can change your real mileage by a lot.
