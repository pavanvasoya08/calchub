Fuel Cost Calculator

Work out how much fuel and money a trip will need from the distance, mileage and fuel price.

About this calculator

Plan fuel costs before a trip or compare vehicles by running cost.

How it is calculated

Fuel needed = distance / mileage. Fuel cost = fuel needed x fuel price. Cost per km = fuel cost / distance.

Example

A 450 km trip at 18 km/l needs 25.00 litres. At ₹105 per litre that costs ₹2,625.00, or ₹5.83 per km.

How to use the Fuel Cost Calculator

  1. Fill in Distance (km), Mileage (km per litre), Fuel price (₹ per litre).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Prices, taxes and tariffs differ from shop to shop and state to state. Enter the actual figures from your bill or price tag.
  • Results are estimates for comparing options and are not a substitute for an official bill.

Frequently asked questions

What mileage should I use?

Use real-world mileage from your trip meter, not the manufacturer claim. City mileage is usually lower than highway mileage.

Does AC use more fuel?

Yes, typically 5-10% more, depending on the vehicle and conditions.

Can I use this for diesel or CNG?

Yes. Enter the mileage and price for the fuel you use.

Is this fuel cost calculator free to use?

Yes. The fuel cost calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
