About this calculator

Probability measures how likely an event is, from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain). This calculator covers simple events and combinations of two independent events.

How it is calculated

P(event) = favourable outcomes / total outcomes. For independent events: P(A and B) = P(A) x P(B). P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A) x P(B). P(not A) = 1 - P(A).

Example

Drawing an ace from a 52-card deck has probability 4 / 52 = 0.0769 (7.69%). If A is 50% and B is 30%, both happen 15% of the time and at least one happens 65% of the time.

How to use the Probability Calculator

  1. Fill in Type, Favourable outcomes, Total outcomes, Probability of A (%), Probability of B (%).

  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The calculator follows standard mathematical definitions. Very large or very small numbers may be shown rounded.
  • Check that your inputs use the units or format the tool asks for (for example, a percentage entered as 12, not 0.12).

Frequently asked questions

What does independent mean?

Two events are independent if one happening does not change the chance of the other, such as two separate coin tosses.

Can a probability be more than 1?

No. Probabilities range from 0 to 1, or 0% to 100%.

What are odds?

Odds compare favourable to unfavourable outcomes. A probability of 4/52 gives odds of 4 to 48.
