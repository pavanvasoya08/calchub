About this calculator

This hours calculator works out how long you worked each day after subtracting your break, then multiplies by the days worked per week. Enter an hourly rate to see your pay.

How it is calculated

Daily hours = (clock out - clock in) - break. Weekly hours = daily hours x days per week. Pay = hours x hourly rate. If clock out is earlier than clock in, the shift is treated as overnight.

Example

09:00 to 17:30 with a 30 minute break is 8 hours a day. Over 5 days that is 40 hours, and at ₹250 an hour the weekly pay is ₹10,000.

How to use the Hours Calculator

  1. Fill in Clock in, Clock out, Unpaid break (minutes), Days worked per week, Hourly rate (₹, optional).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Dates use the Gregorian calendar and your device's local time zone unless the tool says otherwise.
  • Public holidays differ by country and state, so holiday-based results only count the holidays you enter.

Frequently asked questions

Is the break paid?

This calculator treats the break as unpaid and subtracts it.

Can I use it for overnight shifts?

Yes. If clock out is earlier than clock in, the shift runs into the next day.

Does it include overtime?

No. All hours are paid at the single rate you enter.

How do I ignore pay?

Set the hourly rate to 0 and only the hours are shown.

Is this hours calculator free to use?

Yes. The hours calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
