Electricity Bill Calculator

Estimate your electricity bill using slab rates, or work out the running cost of an appliance.

About this calculator

Electricity is billed in units (kWh). Most providers charge a higher rate as consumption rises, so a slab calculation is more accurate than a single rate.

How it is calculated

1 unit = 1 kWh = 1000 watts used for 1 hour. Slab bill = units in each slab x its rate, plus fixed charge and duty. Appliance cost = watts x hours x days x quantity / 1000 x rate.

Example

For 350 units with slabs of ₹3.50, ₹5.00 and ₹7.00 per unit, the energy charge is ₹1,700.00. Adding a ₹50 fixed charge and 5% duty gives a bill of ₹1,837.50. A 1500 W heater for 4 hours a day over 30 days uses 180 kWh.

How to use the Electricity Bill Calculator

  1. Fill in Calculate, Units consumed (kWh), Slab 1 up to (units), Slab 1 rate (₹/unit), Slab 2 up to (units), Slab 2 rate (₹/unit) and 8 more option(s).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Prices, taxes and tariffs differ from shop to shop and state to state. Enter the actual figures from your bill or price tag.
  • Results are estimates for comparing options and are not a substitute for an official bill.

Frequently asked questions

Are the default rates real?

They are sample slabs. Rates differ by state, provider and consumer type, so enter the rates printed on your bill.

How many units is my appliance?

Multiply its watts by hours used and divide by 1000. A 1000 W appliance running for 1 hour uses 1 unit.

Why does my actual bill differ?

Bills may include fuel adjustment charges, meter rent, subsidies or surcharges that are not in this estimate.

Is this electricity bill calculator free to use?

Yes. The electricity bill calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
