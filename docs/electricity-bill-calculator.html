About this calculator

Electricity is billed in units, also called kilowatt-hours (kWh). One kWh is the energy used by a 1,000-watt appliance running for one hour.

How it is calculated

Energy (kWh) = watts × hours per day × days ÷ 1,000. Cost = kWh × tariff. Note that real bills may also include fixed charges and slab rates.

Example

A 1,500 W heater used for 3 hours a day for 30 days uses 135 kWh. At ₹7 per unit, that costs ₹945.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find an appliance's wattage?

Check the label on the appliance or its manual.

Why is my bill different?

Your bill may include slab rates, fixed charges and taxes that this simple calculator does not include.
