About this calculator

ROAS tells you if your advertising is profitable, especially when compared with your break-even point.

How it is calculated

ROAS = ad revenue / ad spend. ACOS = ad spend / ad revenue x 100. Break-even ROAS = 1 / profit margin.

Example

₹20,000 of ads that bring ₹1,00,000 of revenue gives a ROAS of 5.00x and an ACOS of 20%. At a 30% margin the break-even ROAS is 3.33x, so these ads are profitable.

How to use the ROAS Calculator

  1. Fill in Ad spend (₹), Revenue from ads (₹), Profit margin (%).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results depend on the costs, prices and fee rates you enter. Marketplace fees, GST and platform rules change often, so confirm current rates with the platform or your accountant.
  • Figures are estimates for planning and are not accounting, tax or legal advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good ROAS?

A common target is 4x, but the right number depends on your margin. Compare it with the break-even ROAS shown above.

What is the difference between ROAS and ROI?

ROAS looks only at revenue per unit of ad spend. ROI looks at profit after all costs.

What is ACOS?

Advertising cost of sales: ad spend as a percentage of ad revenue. It is the inverse of ROAS.

Is this roas calculator free to use?

Yes. The roas calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
