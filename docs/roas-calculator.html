About this calculator

ROAS tells you if your advertising is profitable, especially when compared with your break-even point.

How it is calculated

ROAS = ad revenue / ad spend. ACOS = ad spend / ad revenue x 100. Break-even ROAS = 1 / profit margin.

Example

₹20,000 of ads that bring ₹1,00,000 of revenue gives a ROAS of 5.00x and an ACOS of 20%. At a 30% margin the break-even ROAS is 3.33x, so these ads are profitable.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good ROAS?

A common target is 4x, but the right number depends on your margin. Compare it with the break-even ROAS shown above.

What is the difference between ROAS and ROI?

ROAS looks only at revenue per unit of ad spend. ROI looks at profit after all costs.

What is ACOS?

Advertising cost of sales: ad spend as a percentage of ad revenue. It is the inverse of ROAS.
