Cost Price Calculator

Find the original cost price when you know the selling price and the profit percentage.

About this calculator

Works backwards from the selling price to the cost price.

How it is calculated

If profit is on cost: cost = selling price / (1 + profit / 100). If profit is on selling price: cost = selling price x (1 - profit / 100).

Example

Selling price ₹1,200 with a 20% profit on cost means a cost of ₹1,000.00 and a profit of ₹200.00.

Frequently asked questions

What if I sold at a loss?

Enter a negative profit percentage, for example -10 for a 10% loss.

Is profit usually on cost or selling price?

In school maths, profit % is on cost. In business reports, margin is on the selling price. Pick the right base above.

Why is the cost not simply price minus percent?

Because the percentage applies to a different base. Dividing by (1 + profit %) gives the correct cost.
