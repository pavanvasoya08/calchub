About this calculator

Work out how much interest an amount earns (or costs) over time. Pick simple interest, where interest is charged only on the principal, or compound interest, where interest is added to the balance and earns more interest.

How it is calculated

Simple: I = P x r x t / 100. Compound: A = P x (1 + r / (100n))^(nt), and interest = A - P, where n is the number of compounding periods per year.

Example

On ₹1,00,000 at 8% for 5 years, simple interest is ₹40,000 while yearly compound interest is ₹46,932.81.

How to use the Interest Calculator

  1. Fill in Interest type, Principal (₹), Interest rate (% per year), Time (years), Compounding.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results use standard formulas and the values you enter. Banks, brokers and tax authorities may apply fees, rounding rules or different compounding, so real figures can differ.
  • Interest rates, tax slabs and investment returns change over time. Market-linked returns (SIP, mutual funds, stocks) are not guaranteed.
  • This tool is for estimation and learning. It is not financial, tax or investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between simple and compound interest?

Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal. Compound interest is calculated on the principal plus interest already added, so it grows faster.

Which is used for savings and loans?

Bank deposits and most loans use compound interest. Some short-term personal loans and bonds use simple interest.

Can I enter months?

Yes. Enter the time in years as a decimal, for example 2.5 for two years and six months.

Is this interest calculator free to use?

Yes. The interest calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
