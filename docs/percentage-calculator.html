About this calculator

Percentages express a number as a part of 100. This tool handles the four most common percentage problems.

How it is calculated

X% of Y = X × Y ÷ 100. X as a percent of Y = X ÷ Y × 100. Percentage change = (Y − X) ÷ X × 100. Increasing Y by X% = Y × (1 + X ÷ 100).

Example

20% of 250 is 50. 50 is 20% of 250. A change from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value and multiply by 100.

What is the difference between percentage and percentage points?

If a rate goes from 10% to 12%, it rises by 2 percentage points, which is a 20% increase.
