About this calculator

Percentages show a part out of 100. This calculator handles the five most common percentage problems, including discounts, markups, marks and growth rates.

How it is calculated

X% of Y = X / 100 x Y. X is what % of Y = X / Y x 100. Percentage change = (new - old) / old x 100. Add a percentage: value x (1 + p/100). Subtract: value x (1 - p/100).

Example

20% of 500 is 20 / 100 x 500 = 100. Going from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase because (100 - 80) / 80 x 100 = 25. Adding 15% to 200 gives 230.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The calculator follows standard mathematical definitions. Very large or very small numbers may be shown rounded.
  • Check that your inputs use the units or format the tool asks for (for example, a percentage entered as 12, not 0.12).

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply by the number. For example 15% of 80 is 0.15 x 80 = 12.

What is the difference between percentage and percentage points?

If a rate rises from 10% to 15%, it rises by 5 percentage points, which is a 50% relative increase.

How do I reverse a percentage increase?

Divide the new value by (1 + p/100). For example, a price of 230 after a 15% increase was 230 / 1.15 = 200 before.
