Salary Calculator (CTC to In-hand)

Find your monthly take-home salary from your annual CTC after PF, professional tax and income tax.

About this calculator

Your CTC (cost to company) is not what reaches your bank account. Employer PF, employee PF, professional tax and income tax are all taken out first. This tool estimates the monthly in-hand amount.

How it is calculated

Gross salary = CTC - Employer PF. PF = 12% of basic (both employer and employee). In-hand = Gross - Employee PF - Professional tax - Income tax. Income tax uses the new regime with the Rs 75,000 standard deduction.

Example

For a CTC of Rs 10,00,000 with 40% basic, the basic is Rs 4,00,000 and PF is Rs 48,000. Gross is Rs 9,52,000, income tax is nil under the 12 lakh rebate, and the monthly in-hand is about Rs 77,000 after Rs 2,400 yearly professional tax.

How to use the Salary Calculator

  1. Fill in Annual CTC (₹), Basic salary (% of CTC), HRA (% of basic), Professional tax (₹ per month).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results use standard formulas and the values you enter. Banks, brokers and tax authorities may apply fees, rounding rules or different compounding, so real figures can differ.
  • Interest rates, tax slabs and investment returns change over time. Market-linked returns (SIP, mutual funds, stocks) are not guaranteed.
  • This tool is for estimation and learning. It is not financial, tax or investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between CTC and in-hand salary?

CTC is the total yearly cost to the employer, including PF and benefits. In-hand salary is the amount credited to your account after all deductions.

Why is my actual salary different from this estimate?

Companies structure salary differently. Bonuses, insurance, gratuity and variable pay change the actual figure. Use this as a close estimate.

Is the tax calculation exact?

It is a simple new-regime estimate with only the standard deduction. For a full comparison use the income tax calculator.

Is this salary calculator free to use?

Yes. The salary calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
