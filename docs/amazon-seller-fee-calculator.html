About this calculator

Shows what is left of each sale after marketplace fees. Rates are editable because they change by category and price.

How it is calculated

Fees = referral fee (price x rate) + closing fee + shipping fee. GST is charged on those fees. Net payout = price - fees - GST on fees. Profit = payout - product cost.

Example

On a ₹1,000 item with 8% referral, ₹20 closing and ₹60 shipping, total fees with 18% GST are ₹188.80. The payout is ₹811.20 and the profit after a ₹500 cost is ₹311.20.

How to use the Amazon Seller Fee Calculator

  1. Fill in Selling price (₹), Referral fee (%), Closing fee (₹), Shipping / FBA fee (₹), GST on fees (%), Product cost (₹).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results depend on the costs, prices and fee rates you enter. Marketplace fees, GST and platform rules change often, so confirm current rates with the platform or your accountant.
  • Figures are estimates for planning and are not accounting, tax or legal advice.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find the exact Amazon fees?

On the fee schedule in Amazon Seller Central. Rates vary by category, price band and fulfilment method.

Are the default rates official?

No, they are illustrative. Replace them with the current rates for your product.

Does the payout include TCS or TDS?

No. Taxes collected at source and returns are not included, so your final payout may differ.

Is this amazon seller fee calculator free to use?

Yes. The amazon seller fee calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
