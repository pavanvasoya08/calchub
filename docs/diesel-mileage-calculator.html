About this calculator

Track your diesel efficiency with the full-tank method: fill up, drive, refill and enter the litres added.

How it is calculated

Mileage (km/l) = distance travelled / fuel used. Fuel per 100 km = 100 / mileage. Cost per km = fuel price x fuel used / distance.

Example

Driving 320 km on 16 litres gives a mileage of 20 km/l and 5 litres per 100 km. At ₹92 per litre the cost is ₹4.60 per km.

How to use the Diesel Mileage Calculator

  1. Fill in Distance travelled (km), Diesel used (litres), Diesel price (₹ per litre).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Prices, taxes and tariffs differ from shop to shop and state to state. Enter the actual figures from your bill or price tag.
  • Results are estimates for comparing options and are not a substitute for an official bill.

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure mileage accurately?

Fill the tank fully, reset the trip meter, drive normally, then refill to the same level. Use the litres added.

What affects mileage?

Driving style, speed, tyre pressure, traffic, load and air conditioning all change it.

What is a good mileage?

For petrol cars 15-20 km/l is good; for diesel cars 18-25 km/l is common; bikes often achieve 40-60 km/l.

Is this diesel mileage calculator free to use?

Yes. The diesel mileage calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
