Travel Cost Calculator

Estimate the total cost of a road trip, including fuel, tolls and other expenses, and split it between travellers.

About this calculator

Plan a trip budget and share costs fairly.

How it is calculated

Fuel cost = distance / mileage x fuel price. Total = fuel + tolls + other costs. Per person = total / travellers.

Example

A 600 km trip at 15 km/l and ₹105 per litre costs ₹4,200 in fuel. With ₹450 tolls and ₹300 other costs, the total is ₹4,950, or ₹1,238 each for 4 people.

How to use the Travel Cost Calculator

  1. Fill in Total distance (km), Mileage (km per litre), Fuel price (₹ per litre), Tolls and parking (₹), Other costs (₹), Number of travellers.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Prices, taxes and tariffs differ from shop to shop and state to state. Enter the actual figures from your bill or price tag.
  • Results are estimates for comparing options and are not a substitute for an official bill.

Frequently asked questions

Should I double the distance for a return trip?

Yes. Enter the full round-trip distance.

Does it include food and stays?

Add them in the Other costs field.

How accurate is the fuel estimate?

It depends on the mileage you enter. Use your real-world mileage for the type of road.

Is this travel cost calculator free to use?

Yes. The travel cost calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
