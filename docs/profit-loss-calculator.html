Profit and Loss Calculator

Enter a cost price and a selling price to see your profit or loss as an amount and a percentage.

About this calculator

Use this tool to check if a sale makes a profit or a loss. It gives the amount, the percentage on cost price, and the margin on selling price.

How it is calculated

Profit = Selling price - Cost price. Profit % = Profit / Cost price x 100. Margin % = Profit / Selling price x 100. If the selling price is lower than the cost price, the same formulas give a loss.

Example

Buying at ₹800 and selling at ₹1,000 gives a profit of ₹200, which is 25% on cost and 20% margin on selling price.

How to use the Profit & Loss Calculator

  1. Fill in Cost price per unit (₹), Selling price per unit (₹), Quantity.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results use standard formulas and the values you enter. Banks, brokers and tax authorities may apply fees, rounding rules or different compounding, so real figures can differ.
  • Interest rates, tax slabs and investment returns change over time. Market-linked returns (SIP, mutual funds, stocks) are not guaranteed.
  • This tool is for estimation and learning. It is not financial, tax or investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between profit % and margin %?

Profit % is based on cost price. Margin % is based on selling price, so it is always lower than profit % for a profitable sale.

How do I calculate loss percentage?

Loss % = (Cost price - Selling price) / Cost price x 100. Enter the lower selling price to see it.

Does this include expenses?

Only the prices you enter. Add shipping, packaging or other costs to the cost price for the true result.

Is this profit & loss calculator free to use?

Yes. The profit & loss calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
