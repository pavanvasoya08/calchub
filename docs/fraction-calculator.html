Fraction Calculator

Add, subtract, multiply or divide two fractions and get the answer in simplest form, as a mixed number and as a decimal.

About this calculator

This calculator works with two fractions and always reduces the answer to its simplest form.

How it is calculated

Add: a/b + c/d = (ad + cb) / bd. Subtract: (ad - cb) / bd. Multiply: ac / bd. Divide: ad / bc. The result is divided by the greatest common divisor to simplify.

Example

1/2 + 3/4 = (1 x 4 + 3 x 2) / (2 x 4) = 10/8, which simplifies to 5/4, or 1 1/4, or 1.25.

How to use the Fraction Calculator

  1. Fill in Fraction 1: numerator, Fraction 1: denominator, Operation, Fraction 2: numerator, Fraction 2: denominator.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The calculator follows standard mathematical definitions. Very large or very small numbers may be shown rounded.
  • Check that your inputs use the units or format the tool asks for (for example, a percentage entered as 12, not 0.12).

Frequently asked questions

How do I add fractions with different denominators?

Find a common denominator, convert both fractions, then add the numerators.

How do I divide fractions?

Flip the second fraction (take its reciprocal) and multiply.

What is a mixed number?

A whole number combined with a proper fraction, such as 1 1/4.

Is this fraction calculator free to use?

Yes. The fraction calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
