About this calculator

CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) is the steady yearly rate at which an investment would have to grow to reach its final value from its starting value. It smooths out year-to-year ups and downs.

How it is calculated

CAGR = (Final value ÷ Initial value)1 ÷ years − 1.

Example

An investment that grows from ₹1,00,000 to ₹2,50,000 in 5 years has a CAGR of 20.11% a year.

Frequently asked questions

Is CAGR the same as average return?

No. Average return is a simple mean of yearly returns, while CAGR accounts for compounding, so it is usually more accurate.

Can CAGR be negative?

Yes. If the final value is lower than the initial value, the CAGR is negative.
