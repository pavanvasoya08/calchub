About this calculator

Convert litres to us gallons instantly. Type a value and the result updates as you type. The calculator also shows the reverse conversion and a table of common values. The US gallon is 3.785 litres and the UK (imperial) gallon is 4.546 litres, so both results are shown.

How it is calculated

US gallons = Litres x 0.2641720524. To go back, divide by 0.2641720524.

Example

10 L = 2.642 gal (US). And 10 gal (US) = 37.854 L.
