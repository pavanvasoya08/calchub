Business Days Calculator

Count the business days between two dates, or add and subtract business days from a date.

About this calculator

Business days are Monday to Friday. Use this calculator to find how many business days lie between two dates, or to find the date that is a certain number of business days from today.

How it is calculated

Counting: every date in the range that is not Saturday or Sunday is counted, then holidays are subtracted. Adding: the calculator steps one day at a time and only counts weekdays until the target number is reached.

Example

Adding 10 business days to Friday 20 November 2026 gives Friday 4 December 2026 because two weekends are skipped.

How to use the Business Days Calculator

  1. Fill in What do you want to do?, Start date, End date, Number of business days, Holidays to subtract.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Dates use the Gregorian calendar and your device's local time zone unless the tool says otherwise.
  • Public holidays differ by country and state, so holiday-based results only count the holidays you enter.

Frequently asked questions

Which days are business days?

Monday to Friday. Saturdays and Sundays are skipped.

Does the count include the start date?

When counting between dates, yes. When adding days, counting starts from the day after the start date.

Are public holidays skipped automatically?

No. Enter the number of holidays in count mode, or add extra days yourself in add mode.

Can I subtract business days?

Yes. Choose 'Subtract business days from a date'.

Is this business days calculator free to use?

Yes. The business days calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
