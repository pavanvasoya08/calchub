About this calculator

The size of a bigha is not the same across India. It changes from state to state, and sometimes between districts. Choose your state to convert acres to bigha, or bigha to acres, with the usual local size. A table shows the same value in every state.

How it is calculated

1 acre = 43,560 square feet. Bigha = acres x 43,560 / square feet per bigha (for your state). Acres = bigha x square feet per bigha / 43,560.

Example

1 acre in Uttar Pradesh (1 bigha = 27,000 sq ft) is 1.613 bigha. In Gujarat (1 bigha = 17,427 sq ft) it is 2.5 bigha.

Frequently asked questions

How many bigha are in an acre?

It depends on the state. In Uttar Pradesh 1 acre is about 1.61 bigha, in Gujarat about 2.5 bigha and in Punjab about 4.8 bigha.

Why does the bigha size change?

Bigha was a traditional local measure and each region fixed its own size. Even within a state the size can differ between districts, so check your local revenue record before any transaction.

How many square feet are in one acre?

1 acre is 43,560 square feet or about 4,047 square metres.

Is this result legally valid?

No. It is an estimate based on commonly used sizes. For registration or sale, use the area written in your land records.
