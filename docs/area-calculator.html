About this calculator

Area is the amount of surface a flat shape covers. It is measured in square units, such as square metres or square feet.

How it is calculated

Rectangle: length × width. Square: side2. Circle: π × radius2. Triangle: ½ × base × height. Parallelogram: base × height. Trapezoid: ½ × (a + b) × height.

Example

A rectangle that is 10 m long and 5 m wide has an area of 50 square metres.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert square feet to square metres?

Multiply square feet by 0.092903. You can also use our area converter.

Why must all units match?

Mixing units, such as feet and inches, gives the wrong answer. Convert everything to one unit first.
