Construction Cost Calculator

Estimate the cost of building a house from built-up area, number of floors and quality of finish.

About this calculator

This is a quick budget estimate for a new house. Pick a quality level or enter your own local rate per square foot, and the calculator returns the total cost with a material and labour split and a typical breakdown by work stage.

How it is calculated

Total cost = built-up area x floors x rate per sq ft. Preset rates are Economy 1,500, Standard 2,000 and Premium 2,800 per sq ft. Material is shown at about 60% and labour at about 40% of the total.

Example

A 1,000 sq ft house at the standard rate of ₹2,000 per sq ft costs ₹20,00,000, roughly ₹12,00,000 for materials and ₹8,00,000 for labour.

How to use the Construction Cost Calculator

  1. Fill in Built-up area per floor (sq ft), Number of floors, Quality of construction, Your own rate (₹ per sq ft, 0 = use quality preset).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Quantities are theoretical estimates. Real projects need extra for wastage, site conditions and design changes.
  • Rates for materials, labour, stamp duty and registration vary by city and change often. Confirm with your contractor, engineer or the local registration office.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is this estimate?

It is a planning figure only. Actual cost depends on the city, soil, design, material brands and the contractor, so get detailed quotes.

What is not included?

Land, approvals, interior furniture, landscaping and taxes are not included.

Which rate should I use?

Ask local contractors for the current rate per sq ft in your area and enter it as your own rate.

Is this construction cost calculator free to use?

Yes. The construction cost calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
