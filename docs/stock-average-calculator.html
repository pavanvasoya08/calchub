Stock Average Calculator

Find your average buying price across many purchases and see the effect of buying more shares.

About this calculator

When you buy the same stock at different prices, your average cost changes. Use this tool to find it and to see how buying more at a lower price brings it down.

How it is calculated

Average price = Total amount invested / Total quantity. Total amount is the sum of price x quantity for each purchase.

Example

50 shares at ₹100 and 40 shares at ₹80 cost ₹8,200 for 90 shares, an average of ₹91.11. Buying 30 more at ₹70 lowers the average to ₹85.83.

Frequently asked questions

What is averaging down?

Buying more shares of a stock after its price falls to lower your average cost. It reduces the break-even level but increases your exposure.

How many purchases can I enter?

Up to five existing purchases plus one new purchase. Leave unused rows at 0.

Does it include brokerage?

No. Add charges to the price if you want a cost-inclusive average.
