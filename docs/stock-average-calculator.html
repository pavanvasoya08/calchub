Stock Average Calculator

Find your average buying price across many purchases and see the effect of buying more shares.

About this calculator

When you buy the same stock at different prices, your average cost changes. Use this tool to find it and to see how buying more at a lower price brings it down.

How it is calculated

Average price = Total amount invested / Total quantity. Total amount is the sum of price x quantity for each purchase.

Example

50 shares at ₹100 and 40 shares at ₹80 cost ₹8,200 for 90 shares, an average of ₹91.11. Buying 30 more at ₹70 lowers the average to ₹85.83.

How to use the Stock Average Calculator

  1. Fill in Purchase 1 price (₹), Purchase 1 quantity, Purchase 2 price (₹), Purchase 2 quantity, Purchase 3 price (₹), Purchase 3 quantity and 6 more option(s).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results use standard formulas and the values you enter. Banks, brokers and tax authorities may apply fees, rounding rules or different compounding, so real figures can differ.
  • Interest rates, tax slabs and investment returns change over time. Market-linked returns (SIP, mutual funds, stocks) are not guaranteed.
  • This tool is for estimation and learning. It is not financial, tax or investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is averaging down?

Buying more shares of a stock after its price falls to lower your average cost. It reduces the break-even level but increases your exposure.

How many purchases can I enter?

Up to five existing purchases plus one new purchase. Leave unused rows at 0.

Does it include brokerage?

No. Add charges to the price if you want a cost-inclusive average.

Is this stock average calculator free to use?

Yes. The stock average calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
