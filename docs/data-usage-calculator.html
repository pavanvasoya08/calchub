Estimate how many gigabytes you use each month from your daily habits, and choose the right data plan.

About this calculator

Enter how many hours a day you spend on each activity and how much data each hour uses. The defaults are typical values: HD video uses about 1.5 GB an hour, video calls about 0.8 GB, music about 0.1 GB and browsing about 0.15 GB.

How it is calculated

Monthly data = sum of (hours per day x GB per hour) x number of days.

Example

2 h of video, 1 h of calls, 2 h of music and 2 h of browsing a day use 4.30 GB a day, or 129.0 GB over 30 days.

How to use the Data Usage Calculator

  1. Fill in Video streaming (hours per day), GB per hour of video, Video calls (hours per day), GB per hour of calls, Music streaming (hours per day), GB per hour of music and 3 more option(s).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Real-world speeds, file sizes and compression vary from the theoretical values used here.
  • Text and password tools run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded or stored.

Frequently asked questions

How much data does streaming use?

Standard definition uses about 0.7 GB an hour, HD about 1.5 to 3 GB and 4K about 7 GB.

How can I use less data?

Lower the video quality, download content over Wi-Fi and turn off auto-play.

Are the defaults accurate?

They are averages. Edit the GB per hour fields if you know your own usage from your phone settings.

Is this data usage calculator free to use?

Yes. The data usage calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
