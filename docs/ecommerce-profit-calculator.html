E-commerce Profit Calculator

Find your true profit per order after platform fees, shipping, packaging, ad cost and returns.

About this calculator

Gives a realistic profit per order by counting costs that sellers often forget, including returns.

How it is calculated

Profit if delivered = price - product cost - platform fee - shipping - packaging - ad cost. A returned order loses shipping, packaging and ad cost. Expected profit = (1 - return rate) x delivered profit + return rate x returned loss.

Example

A ₹999 product with a ₹400 cost makes ₹324.12 per delivered order. With a 10% return rate the expected profit is ₹276.21 per order, or ₹27,621 on 100 orders.

Frequently asked questions

Why include returns?

Returns can wipe out profit, especially in fashion and electronics. Including them avoids over-estimating earnings.

What is a healthy e-commerce margin?

Many sellers aim for at least 15-20% net margin after all costs, but it varies by category.

Do the numbers include GST?

No. Enter the selling price and fees in the form you use for your accounts, and handle GST separately.
