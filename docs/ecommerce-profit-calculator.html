E-commerce Profit Calculator

Find your true profit per order after platform fees, shipping, packaging, ad cost and returns.

About this calculator

Gives a realistic profit per order by counting costs that sellers often forget, including returns.

How it is calculated

Profit if delivered = price - product cost - platform fee - shipping - packaging - ad cost. A returned order loses shipping, packaging and ad cost. Expected profit = (1 - return rate) x delivered profit + return rate x returned loss.

Example

A ₹999 product with a ₹400 cost makes ₹324.12 per delivered order. With a 10% return rate the expected profit is ₹276.21 per order, or ₹27,621 on 100 orders.

How to use the E-commerce Profit Calculator

  1. Fill in Selling price (₹), Product cost (₹), Platform fee (%), Shipping per order (₹), Packaging per order (₹), Ad cost per sale (₹) and 2 more option(s).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results depend on the costs, prices and fee rates you enter. Marketplace fees, GST and platform rules change often, so confirm current rates with the platform or your accountant.
  • Figures are estimates for planning and are not accounting, tax or legal advice.

Frequently asked questions

Why include returns?

Returns can wipe out profit, especially in fashion and electronics. Including them avoids over-estimating earnings.

What is a healthy e-commerce margin?

Many sellers aim for at least 15-20% net margin after all costs, but it varies by category.

Do the numbers include GST?

No. Enter the selling price and fees in the form you use for your accounts, and handle GST separately.

Is this e-commerce profit calculator free to use?

Yes. The e-commerce profit calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
