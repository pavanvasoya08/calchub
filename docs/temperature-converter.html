About this calculator

Celsius is used in most of the world, Fahrenheit in the United States and Kelvin in science. This tool converts between all three.

How it is calculated

°F = °C × 9 ÷ 5 + 32. °C = (°F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9. K = °C + 273.15.

Example

25°C is 77°F. Water freezes at 0°C (32°F) and boils at 100°C (212°F).

Frequently asked questions

What is absolute zero?

Absolute zero is 0 K, which is −273.15°C or −459.67°F. Nothing can be colder.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit quickly?

Double the Celsius value and add 30 for a rough answer.
