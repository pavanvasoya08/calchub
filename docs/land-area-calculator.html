Land Area Calculator

Find the area of a plot of land by shape and see it in square feet, square metres, gaj, acres and hectares.

About this calculator

Land is rarely a perfect rectangle. This calculator supports four common plot shapes and converts the area into the units used in property deals: square feet, square metres, square yards (gaj), acres and hectares.

How it is calculated

Rectangle = L x W. Triangle = 1/2 x base x height. Trapezium = (A + B) / 2 x height. Circle = pi x r^2. 1 acre = 43,560 sq ft, 1 hectare = 10,000 sq m, 1 sq yd (gaj) = 9 sq ft, 1 guntha = 1,089 sq ft.

Example

A rectangular plot of 60 ft x 40 ft has an area of 2,400 sq ft, which is 222.97 sq m, 266.67 sq yd and 0.0551 acres.

How to use the Land Area Calculator

  1. Fill in Plot shape, Measurement unit, Length, Width, Base, Height and 4 more option(s).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Quantities are theoretical estimates. Real projects need extra for wastage, site conditions and design changes.
  • Rates for materials, labour, stamp duty and registration vary by city and change often. Confirm with your contractor, engineer or the local registration office.

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure an irregular plot?

Divide it into rectangles and triangles, find each area with this calculator and add the results.

How many square feet are in an acre?

One acre equals 43,560 square feet, or about 4,047 square metres.

What is a gaj?

Gaj is the Indian name for a square yard and equals 9 square feet.

Is this land area calculator free to use?

Yes. The land area calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
