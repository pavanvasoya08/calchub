Brokerage Calculator

Work out brokerage, STT, taxes and your breakeven price for equity delivery and intraday trades.

About this calculator

Every share trade has brokerage, STT, exchange charges, GST, SEBI fees and stamp duty. These costs reduce your profit, so this calculator shows your real net result and the breakeven selling price.

How it is calculated

Brokerage = Turnover x Rate (capped for intraday). STT is 0.1% on buy and sell for delivery and 0.025% on sell for intraday. Exchange charges and SEBI fees apply on total turnover. GST is 18% of brokerage, exchange and SEBI charges. Stamp duty is 0.015% (delivery) or 0.003% (intraday) on buy.

Example

Buying 100 shares at Rs 500 and selling at Rs 520 gives a gross profit of Rs 2,000. After brokerage, STT, exchange charges, GST, SEBI and stamp duty, the net profit is a little under Rs 1,900 for a delivery trade.

How to use the Brokerage Calculator

  1. Fill in Trade type, Buy price (₹), Sell price (₹), Quantity, Brokerage (% per side), Intraday brokerage cap per order (₹) and 1 more option(s).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results use standard formulas and the values you enter. Banks, brokers and tax authorities may apply fees, rounding rules or different compounding, so real figures can differ.
  • Interest rates, tax slabs and investment returns change over time. Market-linked returns (SIP, mutual funds, stocks) are not guaranteed.
  • This tool is for estimation and learning. It is not financial, tax or investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the breakeven price?

It is the selling price at which your net profit after all charges is zero. Selling above it makes a profit.

Do charges differ between brokers?

Yes. Brokerage varies by broker, while STT, stamp duty and exchange charges are set by regulators and exchanges. Edit the inputs to match your broker.

Are these figures exact?

They are close estimates. Rates change over time, so check your contract note for the final amounts.

Is this brokerage calculator free to use?

Yes. The brokerage calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
