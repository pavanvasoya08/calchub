Concrete Calculator

Work out how much concrete you need and the cement, sand and aggregate to mix, for M15, M20 or M25 grade.

About this calculator

Use this for slabs, beams, columns, footings and plinths: enter the size of one member, how many there are, and the grade. The calculator returns the concrete volume in cubic metres and cubic feet and the dry quantities of each material.

How it is calculated

Volume = L x W x T x number of members, plus wastage. Dry volume = volume x 1.54. Material share follows the nominal mix: M15 = 1:2:4, M20 = 1:1.5:3, M25 = 1:1:2. Cement is converted to 50 kg bags at 1,440 kg/m3.

Example

A 6 m x 4 m slab that is 0.125 m thick holds 3 m3, or 105.94 cu ft. With 5% wastage and M20 mix you need about 25.40 bags of cement.

How to use the Concrete Calculator

  1. Fill in Length (m), Width (m), Depth / thickness (m), Number of identical members, Concrete grade, Wastage (%).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Quantities are theoretical estimates. Real projects need extra for wastage, site conditions and design changes.
  • Rates for materials, labour, stamp duty and registration vary by city and change often. Confirm with your contractor, engineer or the local registration office.

Frequently asked questions

What do M15, M20 and M25 mean?

The number is the characteristic compressive strength in N/mm2 after 28 days. M20 is common for residential slabs and beams, M25 for heavier structural work.

Does this include reinforcement steel?

No. It covers concrete materials only. Steel quantity depends on the structural design.

Is nominal mix suitable for every job?

Nominal mixes are fine for small residential work. Larger structures should use a design mix specified by an engineer.

Is this concrete calculator free to use?

Yes. The concrete calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
