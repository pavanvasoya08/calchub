PPF Calculator

Find the maturity value of your Public Provident Fund account with yearly deposits and compounding interest.

About this calculator

The Public Provident Fund is a government-backed savings scheme with a 15-year lock-in. Interest is tax-free and compounds every year, which makes it a popular long-term savings choice.

How it is calculated

Each year: Interest = (Opening balance + Deposit) x Rate. Closing balance = Opening balance + Deposit + Interest. The yearly deposit is limited to Rs 1,50,000.

Example

Depositing ₹1,50,000 every year for 15 years at 7.1% gives a maturity value of about ₹40,68,209, with ₹22,50,000 invested.

How to use the PPF Calculator

  1. Fill in Yearly deposit (₹, max 1,50,000), Interest rate (% per year), Years (minimum 15).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results use standard formulas and the values you enter. Banks, brokers and tax authorities may apply fees, rounding rules or different compounding, so real figures can differ.
  • Interest rates, tax slabs and investment returns change over time. Market-linked returns (SIP, mutual funds, stocks) are not guaranteed.
  • This tool is for estimation and learning. It is not financial, tax or investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the PPF interest rate?

The government announces the rate every quarter. The default here is 7.1%, but you can change it to match the current rate.

How much can I deposit in PPF each year?

You can deposit between Rs 500 and Rs 1,50,000 in a financial year.

Can I extend PPF after 15 years?

Yes, the account can be extended in blocks of 5 years, with or without fresh deposits. Enter a longer period to see the effect.

Is this ppf calculator free to use?

Yes. The ppf calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
