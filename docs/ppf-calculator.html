About this calculator

The Public Provident Fund (PPF) is a long-term, government-backed savings scheme in India. The interest rate is set by the government and reviewed regularly, so enter the current rate before you calculate.

How it is calculated

Each year: closing balance = (opening balance + yearly deposit) × (1 + rate). This assumes the deposit is made at the start of each financial year.

Example

Depositing ₹1,50,000 every year for 15 years at 7.1% gives a maturity value of about ₹40,68,209.

Frequently asked questions

What is the PPF lock-in period?

The standard PPF tenure is 15 years, and it can be extended in blocks of 5 years. Check the latest rules for partial withdrawals.

Is PPF interest taxable?

Under current rules PPF has been treated as tax-exempt, but tax laws change. Please verify with an official source.
