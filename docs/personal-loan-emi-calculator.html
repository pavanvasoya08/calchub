About this calculator

This personal loan emi calculator works out your monthly instalment, the total interest you will pay and the total repayment. A year-by-year table shows how much of your payments goes to principal and interest.

How it is calculated

EMI = P x i x (1+i)^n / ((1+i)^n - 1), where P = loan amount, i = annual rate / 12 / 100 and n = tenure in months.

Example

A loan of ₹3,00,000 at 12% for 3 years has an EMI of ₹9,964. You repay ₹3,58,715 in total, of which ₹58,715 is interest.

How to use the Personal Loan EMI Calculator

  1. Fill in Loan amount (₹), Interest rate (% per year), Loan tenure (years).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results use standard formulas and the values you enter. Banks, brokers and tax authorities may apply fees, rounding rules or different compounding, so real figures can differ.
  • Interest rates, tax slabs and investment returns change over time. Market-linked returns (SIP, mutual funds, stocks) are not guaranteed.
  • This tool is for estimation and learning. It is not financial, tax or investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

How is EMI calculated?

EMI = P x i x (1+i)^n / ((1+i)^n - 1), where P is the loan amount, i is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly instalments.

Does a longer tenure reduce my EMI?

Yes, the monthly EMI falls, but you pay much more interest over the life of the loan. Choose the shortest tenure you can comfortably afford.

Does the calculator include processing fees?

No. It shows only principal and interest. Processing fees, insurance and other charges are extra, so ask your lender for the full cost.

Is this personal loan emi calculator free to use?

Yes. The personal loan emi calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
