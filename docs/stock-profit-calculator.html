Stock Profit Calculator

Calculate your profit or loss and return percentage on a share trade, including charges.

Advertisement

About this calculator

Enter your buy price, sell price and quantity to see the gain or loss on a trade. Add an estimate of charges to see the net result.

How it is calculated

Gross profit = (Sell price - Buy price) x Quantity. Net profit = Gross profit - Charges. Return % = Net profit / Invested amount x 100.

Example

Buying 100 shares at ₹250 and selling at ₹290 gives a gross profit of ₹4,000. Charges of 0.1% on ₹54,000 are ₹54, so the net profit is ₹3,946, a return of 15.78%.

How to use the Stock Profit Calculator

  1. Fill in Buy price (₹), Sell price (₹), Quantity, Total charges (% of buy + sell value).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results use standard formulas and the values you enter. Banks, brokers and tax authorities may apply fees, rounding rules or different compounding, so real figures can differ.
  • Interest rates, tax slabs and investment returns change over time. Market-linked returns (SIP, mutual funds, stocks) are not guaranteed.
  • This tool is for estimation and learning. It is not financial, tax or investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

What charges should I include?

Brokerage, STT, exchange fees, GST, SEBI fees and stamp duty. Around 0.1% is a rough figure for delivery trades. Use the brokerage calculator for an exact split.

Is profit on shares taxed?

Yes. Short-term and long-term capital gains tax rules apply depending on the holding period. This tool does not calculate tax.

Can I use it for losses?

Yes. If the sell price is lower than the buy price the result shows a negative number.

Is this stock profit calculator free to use?

Yes. The stock profit calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
