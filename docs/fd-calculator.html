About this calculator

A fixed deposit (FD) pays a fixed rate of interest for a fixed period. Most banks compound FD interest every quarter, so interest also earns interest.

How it is calculated

Maturity amount A = P × (1 + r ÷ (100 × n))n × t, where n is the number of compounding periods per year and t is the number of years.

Example

₹1,00,000 at 7% for 5 years with quarterly compounding matures to about ₹1,41,478.

Frequently asked questions

Is FD interest taxable?

In most countries, including India, interest from fixed deposits is taxable as income. This calculator does not deduct tax.

Which compounding frequency is best?

More frequent compounding gives slightly higher returns, but the difference is usually small.
