Final Price Calculator

Apply up to three successive discounts and tax to see the final price you will pay.

About this calculator

Stacked offers, such as a sale plus a coupon, apply one after the other, so the total discount is not simply the sum.

How it is calculated

Each discount is applied to the price left by the previous one. Final price = price after discounts x (1 + tax / 100).

Example

₹5,000 less 20% is ₹4,000; less another 10% is ₹3,600. With 18% tax the final price is ₹4,248. The effective discount is 28%, not 30%.

Frequently asked questions

Why is 20% + 10% not 30%?

The second discount applies to the already reduced price, so the total is 28%.

Can I use only one discount?

Yes. Leave the other discount fields at zero.

Which tax rate should I enter?

Use the GST or sales tax rate for the item, for example 18% GST. Enter 0 if the price is tax-inclusive.
