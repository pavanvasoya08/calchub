About this calculator

Stacked offers, such as a sale plus a coupon, apply one after the other, so the total discount is not simply the sum.

How it is calculated

Each discount is applied to the price left by the previous one. Final price = price after discounts x (1 + tax / 100).

Example

₹5,000 less 20% is ₹4,000; less another 10% is ₹3,600. With 18% tax the final price is ₹4,248. The effective discount is 28%, not 30%.

How to use the Final Price Calculator

  1. Fill in Original price (₹), Discount 1 (%), Discount 2 (%), Discount 3 (%), Tax (%).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Prices, taxes and tariffs differ from shop to shop and state to state. Enter the actual figures from your bill or price tag.
  • Results are estimates for comparing options and are not a substitute for an official bill.

Frequently asked questions

Why is 20% + 10% not 30%?

The second discount applies to the already reduced price, so the total is 28%.

Can I use only one discount?

Yes. Leave the other discount fields at zero.

Which tax rate should I enter?

Use the GST or sales tax rate for the item, for example 18% GST. Enter 0 if the price is tax-inclusive.

Is this final price calculator free to use?

Yes. The final price calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
