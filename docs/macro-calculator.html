About this calculator

Macronutrients are carbohydrates, protein and fat. This calculator turns your calorie goal into daily gram targets for each.

How it is calculated

Grams = calories x share / calories per gram. Carbohydrate and protein provide 4 kcal per gram, fat provides 9 kcal per gram.

Example

On 2,200 kcal with a balanced 40/30/30 split you need 220 g carbohydrates, 165 g protein and 73 g fat.

How to use the Macro Calculator

  1. Fill in Maintenance calories per day, Goal, Diet style.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results are general estimates based on population formulas and do not replace a medical examination.
  • Age, genetics, muscle mass, medical conditions and medication can all change what is healthy for you.
  • This calculator is for information only and is not medical advice. Please consult a doctor or a registered dietitian before making health decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Which split is best?

There is no single best split. Choose one you can follow consistently and make sure protein is adequate.

Where do I get my maintenance calories?

Use our TDEE or Calorie Calculator to estimate it.

Is this calculator a substitute for medical advice?

No. It uses standard formulas to give a general estimate. Individual needs vary, so please consult a doctor or registered dietitian for personal advice.

Is this macro calculator free to use?

Yes. The macro calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
