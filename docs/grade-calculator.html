Grade Calculator

Convert a percentage to a letter grade, and find the score you need on your final exam to reach a target grade.

About this calculator

Enter a percentage to see its letter grade. Use the second part to find the score you need in your final exam to reach a target overall grade.

How it is calculated

Needed score = (target - current x (1 - weight)) / weight, where weight is the final exam's share as a decimal.

Example

With a current grade of 70%, a final exam worth 40% and a target of 80%, you need (80 - 70 x 0.6) / 0.4 = 95% on the final exam. A percentage of 78 is a grade B.

How to use the Grade Calculator

  1. Fill in Your percentage (%), Current course grade (%), Weight of final exam (%), Target course grade (%).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Grading rules differ between boards, universities and countries. Use your institution's official conversion where one exists.
  • Results are for guidance and do not replace an official marksheet or transcript.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate the grade I need?

Use the formula above, or enter your current grade, the exam weight and your target.

Why does my school give different grades?

Grade bands differ between schools and boards. The bands shown here are a common default.

What if the needed score is over 100%?

The target is not reachable with that exam alone, so aim for a lower target or look for extra credit.

Is this grade calculator free to use?

Yes. The grade calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
