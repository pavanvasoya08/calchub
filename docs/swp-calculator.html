About this calculator

A Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) lets you take a fixed amount out of an investment every month while the remaining balance keeps earning returns. It is often used to create regular income from savings.

How it is calculated

Each month the withdrawal is taken first, and the remaining balance grows by the monthly rate: balance = (balance − withdrawal) × (1 + monthly rate).

Example

If your withdrawals are larger than the returns your balance earns, the money will eventually run out. The calculator shows exactly when.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make my money last longer?

Withdraw less each month or hope for higher returns. A safe withdrawal is usually close to what the investment can earn.

Are SWP returns guaranteed?

No. Market returns vary, so use a conservative rate.
