SWP Calculator

See how long your investment lasts and what is left when you withdraw a fixed amount every month.

About this calculator

A Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) lets you take a fixed amount from your investment each month while the rest stays invested. This calculator shows the final balance and whether your money lasts.

How it is calculated

Every month: balance = (balance - withdrawal) x (1 + monthly return), where the monthly return is the annual return divided by 12. The process repeats for every month of the period.

Example

A corpus of ₹20,00,000 with ₹15,000 withdrawn each month at 8% a year is checked month by month, and the table shows the balance at the end of every year.

How to use the SWP Calculator

  1. Fill in Total investment (₹), Monthly withdrawal (₹), Expected return (% per year), Withdrawal period (years).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results use standard formulas and the values you enter. Banks, brokers and tax authorities may apply fees, rounding rules or different compounding, so real figures can differ.
  • Interest rates, tax slabs and investment returns change over time. Market-linked returns (SIP, mutual funds, stocks) are not guaranteed.
  • This tool is for estimation and learning. It is not financial, tax or investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is an SWP?

It is a facility in mutual funds that pays you a fixed amount regularly by selling some units. It is often used to create a monthly income.

How do I make my money last longer?

Withdraw less each month, or invest for a higher return. A withdrawal rate of about 4% a year of the corpus is often used as a rough guide.

Is the withdrawal taxable?

Gains in the amount withdrawn may be taxable as capital gains. Check the current rules or ask a tax adviser.

Is this swp calculator free to use?

Yes. The swp calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
