About this calculator

This calculator finds your exact age as of any date, and also shows your age in months, weeks, days and hours.

How it is calculated

Age is the difference between the two dates. Years and months are counted first, and the remaining days are shown last.

Example

Someone born on 15 June 1995 is 30 years, 5 months and 6 days old on 21 November 2025, which is 11,117 days.

Frequently asked questions

How is age calculated?

The calculator subtracts the date of birth from the chosen date, counting complete years, then months, then days.

Can I find my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the second date to any date you like.

Does it handle leap years?

Yes. Leap days are included when counting total days, weeks and hours.
