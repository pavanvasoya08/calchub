About this calculator

Many schools and universities use a 10-point CGPA. To convert it to a percentage, most apply a multiplier, and the most common one is 9.5.

How it is calculated

Percentage = CGPA × multiplier. To go the other way, CGPA = percentage ÷ multiplier.

Example

A CGPA of 8.4 with the 9.5 multiplier is 79.8%.

Frequently asked questions

Which multiplier should I use?

Use the one your board, university or employer specifies. Different institutions follow different rules.

Is this an official conversion?

No. It is a calculator for guidance. Official conversions come from your institution.
