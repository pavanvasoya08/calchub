CGPA to Percentage Calculator

Convert your CGPA on a 10-point scale to a percentage using the CBSE multiplier of 9.5 or your own.

About this calculator

Many schools and universities convert CGPA to a percentage by multiplying by a fixed number. CBSE uses 9.5.

How it is calculated

Percentage = CGPA x multiplier.

Example

A CGPA of 8.5 with the 9.5 multiplier gives 8.5 x 9.5 = 80.75%.

How to use the CGPA to Percentage Calculator

  1. Fill in CGPA (out of 10), Multiplier.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Grading rules differ between boards, universities and countries. Use your institution's official conversion where one exists.
  • Results are for guidance and do not replace an official marksheet or transcript.

Frequently asked questions

What is the CBSE formula?

Multiply the CGPA by 9.5 to get an approximate percentage.

Which multiplier should I use?

CBSE uses 9.5. Many universities use 10 or their own formula, so check your institution's rules before submitting the result anywhere official.

Is the converted percentage official?

No. It is an approximation. Use the percentage on your official marksheet where one is provided.

Is this cgpa to percentage calculator free to use?

Yes. The cgpa to percentage calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
