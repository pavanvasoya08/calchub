Tax Calculator

Add sales tax to a price or remove it from a tax-inclusive price. This is a sales tax calculator, not an income tax tool.

About this calculator

Works out the tax on a purchase and the price with or without tax.

How it is calculated

Price with tax = price x (1 + rate / 100). Price before tax = price with tax / (1 + rate / 100).

Example

Adding 8% tax to ₹1,000 gives ₹1,080 (tax ₹80). Removing 8% tax from ₹1,080 gives ₹1,000.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the income tax calculator?

No. For income tax see the Income Tax Calculator in the Finance category.

What is the difference between tax-inclusive and exclusive?

Exclusive prices do not include tax. Inclusive prices already include it, so tax must be removed to find the base price.

Does this cover GST?

Yes, enter the GST rate. For a CGST/SGST split use the GST Inclusive/Exclusive Calculator.
