About this calculator

This volume converter converts values between common units instantly. It also shows the value in every unit at once.

How it is calculated

Each unit is converted through a common base unit: value × (from-unit factor) ÷ (to-unit factor). US customary units are used for gallons, cups and ounces.

Example

A US gallon is 3.785 liters.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the volume converter?

It uses exact or internationally accepted conversion factors, with results shown to several decimal places.

Can I convert in either direction?

Yes. Pick any unit in the From and To lists.
