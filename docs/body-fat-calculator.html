About this calculator

Body fat percentage tells you how much of your weight is fat. The U.S. Navy method estimates it from your height and circumference measurements, with no special equipment.

How it is calculated

For men: 495 ÷ (1.0324 − 0.19077 × log10(waist − neck) + 0.15456 × log10(height)) − 450. Women's formula also includes the hip measurement.

Example

Measure your waist at the navel, your neck below the larynx and your hips at the widest point. Use a flexible tape and keep it snug but not tight.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the Navy method?

It is an estimate and can differ from DEXA scans or skinfold tests by a few percentage points.

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

Healthy ranges vary by age and sex. Generally, men range from 10% to 20% and women from 18% to 28%. Ask a professional for personal advice.
