Body Fat Calculator

Estimate your body fat percentage from simple tape measurements using the US Navy method.

About this calculator

This calculator estimates body fat from your height, neck and waist (and hip for women). It uses the US Navy circumference method, which needs only a tape measure.

How it is calculated

Men: 86.010 x log10(waist - neck) - 70.041 x log10(height) + 36.76. Women: 163.205 x log10(waist + hip - neck) - 97.684 x log10(height) - 78.387. All measures in cm. Waist is taken at the navel for men and the narrowest point for women.

Example

A man who is 175 cm tall with a 38 cm neck and 90 cm waist has about 27.2% body fat.

How to use the Body Fat Calculator

  1. Fill in Sex, Height (cm), Neck circumference (cm), Waist circumference (cm), Hip circumference (cm), Body weight (kg), optional.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results are general estimates based on population formulas and do not replace a medical examination.
  • Age, genetics, muscle mass, medical conditions and medication can all change what is healthy for you.
  • This calculator is for information only and is not medical advice. Please consult a doctor or a registered dietitian before making health decisions.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the Navy method?

It is usually within a few percentage points of other methods, but careful, consistent measurements matter.

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

Roughly 14 to 24% for men and 21 to 31% for women is considered average to fit, but healthy ranges vary with age.

Is this calculator a substitute for medical advice?

No. It uses standard formulas to give a general estimate. Individual needs vary, so please consult a doctor or registered dietitian for personal advice.

Is this body fat calculator free to use?

Yes. The body fat calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
