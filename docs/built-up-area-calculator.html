Built-up Area Calculator

Add wall thickness and balcony to the carpet area to get the built-up area, and the super built-up area with common-area loading.

About this calculator

Built-up area is the carpet area plus the area taken by walls, and usually balconies. Adding a share of common areas gives the super built-up area that builders use for pricing.

How it is calculated

Built-up area = carpet x (1 + wall %) + balcony. Super built-up area = built-up x (1 + loading %).

Example

Carpet 900 sq ft with 12% walls and a 60 sq ft balcony gives 1,068.00 sq ft built-up. With 20% loading, super built-up is 1,281.60 sq ft.

How to use the Built-up Area Calculator

  1. Fill in Carpet area (sq ft), Wall area as % of carpet, Balcony / terrace area (sq ft), Common-area loading (%).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Quantities are theoretical estimates. Real projects need extra for wastage, site conditions and design changes.
  • Rates for materials, labour, stamp duty and registration vary by city and change often. Confirm with your contractor, engineer or the local registration office.

Frequently asked questions

Is a balcony part of built-up area?

Usually yes. Balconies are counted in built-up area, though some builders count only part of them.

How thick are walls in the calculation?

Walls typically add 10% to 15% of the carpet area, depending on wall thickness and layout.

Which area should I pay for?

Compare prices on the carpet area, because it is the space you actually use.

Is this built-up area calculator free to use?

Yes. The built-up area calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
