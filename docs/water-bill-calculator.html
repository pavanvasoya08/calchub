Water Bill Calculator

Estimate a water bill from your usage in kilolitres using editable slab rates and a sewage charge.

About this calculator

Municipal water is often billed per kilolitre (1,000 litres) with higher rates for higher use.

How it is calculated

Water charge = kilolitres in each slab x slab rate. Sewage = water charge x sewage %. Total = water + sewage + fixed charge.

Example

For 25 kL with slabs of ₹10, ₹15 and ₹25 per kL, the water charge is ₹375. With 30% sewage (₹112.50) and a ₹50 fixed charge, the total is ₹537.50.

Frequently asked questions

How many litres are in a kilolitre?

1 kilolitre equals 1,000 litres, or 1 cubic metre.

Where do I find my slab rates?

On your water bill or your municipal corporation's website. Rates vary by city.

How much water does a household use?

A typical urban household uses about 3-6 kL a month per person, depending on habits and supply.
