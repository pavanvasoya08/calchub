About this calculator

Simple interest is charged only on the original amount. It does not grow on itself, so the interest is the same every year.

How it is calculated

Simple interest = P x R x T / 100, and Total amount = P + Simple interest, where R is the yearly rate in percent and T is time in years.

Example

₹1,00,000 at 8% for 5 years earns ₹40,000 in interest, for a total of ₹1,40,000.

How to use the Simple Interest Calculator

  1. Fill in Principal (₹), Interest rate (% per year), Time (years).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results use standard formulas and the values you enter. Banks, brokers and tax authorities may apply fees, rounding rules or different compounding, so real figures can differ.
  • Interest rates, tax slabs and investment returns change over time. Market-linked returns (SIP, mutual funds, stocks) are not guaranteed.
  • This tool is for estimation and learning. It is not financial, tax or investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the simple interest formula?

SI = P x R x T / 100. Multiply the principal by the rate and the time in years, then divide by 100.

How do I use months?

Convert months to years by dividing by 12. For 18 months, enter 1.5.

Is simple interest lower than compound interest?

Yes, for the same rate and time. Compound interest adds interest to the balance, so it grows faster.

Is this simple interest calculator free to use?

Yes. The simple interest calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
