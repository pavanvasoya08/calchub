About this calculator

Simple interest is charged only on the original amount. It does not grow on itself, so the interest is the same every year.

How it is calculated

Simple interest = P x R x T / 100, and Total amount = P + Simple interest, where R is the yearly rate in percent and T is time in years.

Example

₹1,00,000 at 8% for 5 years earns ₹40,000 in interest, for a total of ₹1,40,000.

Frequently asked questions

What is the simple interest formula?

SI = P x R x T / 100. Multiply the principal by the rate and the time in years, then divide by 100.

How do I use months?

Convert months to years by dividing by 12. For 18 months, enter 1.5.

Is simple interest lower than compound interest?

Yes, for the same rate and time. Compound interest adds interest to the balance, so it grows faster.
