About this calculator

Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal, not on interest already earned. It is commonly used for short-term loans and basic savings schemes.

How it is calculated

Simple Interest = P × R × T ÷ 100, where P is the principal, R is the annual rate in percent and T is the time in years. Total amount = P + SI.

Example

The simple interest on ₹10,000 at 8% for 3 years is ₹2,400, so the total amount is ₹12,400.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate interest for months?

Convert months to years by dividing by 12. For example, 6 months is 0.5 years.

Is simple interest cheaper than compound interest?

For a borrower, simple interest costs less over long periods because interest does not pile up on interest.
