About this calculator

Character limits are everywhere: SMS messages, tweets, meta descriptions and form fields. Paste your text to see the exact count, with and without spaces.

How it is calculated

Characters with spaces counts every character including spaces and line breaks. Characters without spaces ignores all whitespace. Letters and digits are counted separately.

Frequently asked questions

How many characters fit in a tweet?

A standard post allows 280 characters.

What is the ideal meta description length?

Around 150 to 160 characters so it is not cut off in search results.

Do emojis count as one character?

They can count as more than one in some systems, so leave a small safety margin.
