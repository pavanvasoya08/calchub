Estimate your income tax under the new and old regimes and see which one saves more.

About this calculator

This calculator compares your tax under the new regime (default) and the old regime for FY 2026-27 (AY 2027-28). Deductions in the form only apply to the old regime.

How it is calculated

New regime slabs: up to 4 lakh nil, 4-8 lakh 5%, 8-12 lakh 10%, 12-16 lakh 15%, 16-20 lakh 20%, 20-24 lakh 25%, above 24 lakh 30%. Standard deduction is Rs 75,000, and the 87A rebate makes income up to Rs 12 lakh tax-free. Old regime slabs: up to 2.5 lakh nil, 2.5-5 lakh 5%, 5-10 lakh 20%, above 10 lakh 30%, with a Rs 50,000 standard deduction. Cess of 4% is added in both.

Example

On a gross income of Rs 15,00,000 the new regime taxable income is Rs 14,25,000. Tax on slabs is Rs 90,000 (Rs 20,000 + Rs 40,000 + Rs 30,000), and with 4% cess the total is Rs 93,600.

How to use the Income Tax Calculator

  1. Fill in Annual gross income (₹), Age group (old regime), 80C investments (₹, max 1,50,000), 80D health insurance (₹), NPS 80CCD(1B) (₹, max 50,000), Home loan interest (₹, max 2,00,000) and 2 more option(s).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results use standard formulas and the values you enter. Banks, brokers and tax authorities may apply fees, rounding rules or different compounding, so real figures can differ.
  • Interest rates, tax slabs and investment returns change over time. Market-linked returns (SIP, mutual funds, stocks) are not guaranteed.
  • This tool is for estimation and learning. It is not financial, tax or investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

Is income up to Rs 12 lakh really tax-free?

Under the new regime, the 87A rebate makes tax nil when taxable income is up to Rs 12 lakh. With the Rs 75,000 standard deduction, a salaried person with an income of Rs 12.75 lakh pays no tax.

Which regime is better?

The new regime usually wins unless your total deductions are large (for example 80C, HRA, home loan interest and health insurance together). This calculator shows both so you can compare.

Does the result include surcharge?

No. Surcharge applies only on very high incomes, above Rs 50 lakh. Always confirm the final figure with the Income Tax Department or a tax adviser.

Is this income tax calculator free to use?

Yes. The income tax calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
