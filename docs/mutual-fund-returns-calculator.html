About this calculator

Estimate how much a mutual fund investment can grow, or measure the yearly growth rate you earned. Returns from mutual funds are market-linked, so these are estimates and not guarantees.

How it is calculated

Lump sum: Future value = Amount x (1 + Rate)^Years. SIP: instalments are compounded monthly at the equivalent monthly rate. CAGR = (Final value / Invested)^(1 / Years) - 1.

Example

A lump sum of ₹1,00,000 at 12% for 10 years grows to about ₹3,10,585. A SIP of ₹10,000 a month at 12% for 10 years grows to about ₹22,40,359 on ₹12,00,000 invested.

Frequently asked questions

What is CAGR?

Compound annual growth rate is the constant yearly rate that takes an investment from its start value to its end value over a period.

Are mutual fund returns guaranteed?

No. Returns depend on market performance. Past returns do not guarantee future results.

What is the difference between lump sum and SIP?

A lump sum invests the whole amount at once. A SIP invests a fixed amount every month, which spreads your purchase price over time.
