GST Inclusive/Exclusive Calculator

Convert between GST-exclusive and GST-inclusive prices and see the CGST, SGST or IGST split.

About this calculator

Use it on invoices to get the base price, total GST and the tax split for either kind of supply.

How it is calculated

GST-exclusive to inclusive: final = base x (1 + rate / 100). Inclusive to exclusive: base = final / (1 + rate / 100). Within a state, GST splits equally into CGST and SGST; between states it is IGST.

Example

Adding 18% GST to ₹5,000 gives ₹5,900. The GST is ₹900, split into ₹450 CGST and ₹450 SGST.

How to use the GST Inclusive/Exclusive Calculator

  1. Fill in Amount (₹), GST rate, Amount is, Supply type.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Results depend on the costs, prices and fee rates you enter. Marketplace fees, GST and platform rules change often, so confirm current rates with the platform or your accountant.
  • Figures are estimates for planning and are not accounting, tax or legal advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

CGST goes to the central government and SGST to the state when a sale is within one state. IGST is charged on sales between states or imports.

Which GST rate applies to my product?

It depends on the HSN code of the item. Check the official GST rate finder or ask your accountant.

Is there a separate GST calculator?

Yes, the main GST Calculator in the Finance category does the same maths with a simpler form.
