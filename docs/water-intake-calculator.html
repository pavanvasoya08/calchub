About this calculator

Staying hydrated helps energy, digestion and concentration. Your water need rises with body weight, exercise and heat.

How it is calculated

Base need = 33 ml per kg of body weight. Add about 350 ml for every 30 minutes of exercise and 0.5 to 1 litre in warm or hot weather.

Example

A 70 kg person has a base need of 2.3 litres. With 30 minutes of exercise in moderate weather that becomes 2.7 litres a day.

Frequently asked questions

Do tea, coffee and food count?

Yes. Most drinks and water-rich foods such as fruit and soup contribute to your daily fluid intake.

How do I know if I am drinking enough?

Pale yellow urine and rarely feeling thirsty are good signs.

Is this calculator a substitute for medical advice?

No. It uses standard formulas to give a general estimate. Individual needs vary, so please consult a doctor or registered dietitian for personal advice.
