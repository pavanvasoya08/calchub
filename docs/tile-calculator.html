About this calculator

Select your tile size, enter the room dimensions and set the wastage. The calculator divides the floor area by the area of one tile, adds wastage and rounds up to full tiles and full boxes.

How it is calculated

Tiles = ceil(floor area x (1 + wastage %) / tile area). Boxes = ceil(tiles / tiles per box). A 600 x 600 mm tile covers 3.875 sq ft.

Example

A 12 ft x 10 ft room is 120 sq ft. A 600 x 600 mm tile covers 3.875 sq ft, so you need 35 tiles with 10% wastage, which is 9 boxes of 4.

How to use the Tile Calculator

  1. Fill in Room length (ft), Room width (ft), Tile size (mm), Wastage (%), Tiles per box.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Quantities are theoretical estimates. Real projects need extra for wastage, site conditions and design changes.
  • Rates for materials, labour, stamp duty and registration vary by city and change often. Confirm with your contractor, engineer or the local registration office.

Frequently asked questions

How much wastage should I add for tiles?

10% is usual for straight layouts and 15% for diagonal or patterned layouts.

Should I buy all tiles from the same batch?

Yes. Colour and size can vary slightly between batches, so order everything together.

Does the calculator include grout and adhesive?

No. It covers tiles only. Adhesive and grout are bought separately based on the tile area.

Is this tile calculator free to use?

Yes. The tile calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
