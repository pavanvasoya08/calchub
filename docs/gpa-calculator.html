About this calculator

Grade point average (GPA) is the weighted average of your course grades on a 4.0 scale. Courses with more credit hours count more.

How it is calculated

GPA = sum of (grade points x credits) / total credits. A = 4.0, A- = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, and so on.

Example

Courses: A (4 credits), A- (3 credits), B+ (4 credits), B (3 credits). Quality points = 16 + 11.1 + 13.2 + 9 = 49.3 over 14 credits, so GPA = 3.52.

How to use the GPA Calculator

  1. Fill in Course 1 grade, Course 1 credits, Course 2 grade, Course 2 credits, Course 3 grade, Course 3 credits and 6 more option(s).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Grading rules differ between boards, universities and countries. Use your institution's official conversion where one exists.
  • Results are for guidance and do not replace an official marksheet or transcript.

Frequently asked questions

How is GPA calculated?

Multiply each grade point by the course credits, add them up and divide by total credits.

What is a good GPA?

3.0 or higher is generally considered good, and 3.5 or higher is strong, though expectations differ by program.

Can I include courses I want to estimate?

Yes. Enter the grade you expect and see how it changes your GPA.

Is this gpa calculator free to use?

Yes. The gpa calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
