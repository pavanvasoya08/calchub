About this calculator

Convert centimetres to feet and inches, which is handy for height. The calculator also shows the reverse conversion and a table of common values.

How it is calculated

Feet = cm / 30.48. For feet and inches, take the whole number of feet and convert the remainder: inches = remainder x 12.

Example

170 cm is 5.5774 feet, which is 5 ft 6.9 in.

How to use the CM to Feet Calculator

  1. Fill in Centimetres (cm).

  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
