About this calculator

Shows what everyone owes when the bill includes tax and a tip.

How it is calculated

Total = bill + bill x tax % + bill x tip %. Each person pays total / number of people.

Example

A ₹3,600 bill plus 5% tax (₹180) and a 10% tip (₹360) is ₹4,140. Between 3 people, each pays ₹1,380.

How to use the Split Bill Calculator

  1. Fill in Bill before tax (₹), Tax (%), Tip (%), Number of people.
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Prices, taxes and tariffs differ from shop to shop and state to state. Enter the actual figures from your bill or price tag.
  • Results are estimates for comparing options and are not a substitute for an official bill.

Frequently asked questions

What if people ordered different amounts?

This tool splits equally. For uneven shares, calculate each person's items separately and add their share of tax and tip.

Is the tip calculated on the bill with tax?

Here it is calculated on the pre-tax bill, which is the most common practice.

Can I split by two people?

Yes. Set the number of people to 2.

Is this split bill calculator free to use?

Yes. The split bill calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
