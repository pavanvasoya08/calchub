Flooring Cost Calculator

Estimate the total cost of new flooring from the area, material rate, labour rate and wastage.

About this calculator

Flooring cost has three parts: material (which must include wastage), labour, and extras such as skirting and adhesive. Enter your local rates to get the total and the effective rate per square foot.

How it is calculated

Material cost = area x (1 + wastage %) x material rate. Labour cost = area x labour rate. Total = material + labour + other costs.

Example

For 500 sq ft at ₹80 material and ₹25 labour per sq ft with 10% wastage and ₹5,000 extras: material ₹44,000, labour ₹12,500, total ₹61,500.

How to use the Flooring Cost Calculator

  1. Fill in Floor area (sq ft), Material rate (₹ per sq ft), Labour rate (₹ per sq ft), Wastage (%), Other costs (₹, skirting, adhesive).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Quantities are theoretical estimates. Real projects need extra for wastage, site conditions and design changes.
  • Rates for materials, labour, stamp duty and registration vary by city and change often. Confirm with your contractor, engineer or the local registration office.

Frequently asked questions

Is labour charged on area with or without wastage?

Labour is normally charged on the laid area, so wastage is not added to it.

What is a typical flooring rate?

Rates vary widely by city and material. Get two or three local quotes and enter them here to compare.

Does this include removing old flooring?

No. Add the removal and levelling cost under other costs.

Is this flooring cost calculator free to use?

Yes. The flooring cost calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
