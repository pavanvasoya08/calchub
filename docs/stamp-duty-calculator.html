Stamp Duty Calculator

Calculate the stamp duty on a property purchase, with different rates for male and female buyers in some states.

About this calculator

Stamp duty is a state tax paid when a property is transferred. Some states charge a lower rate when the buyer is a woman. Pick your state, or enter your own rate, to see the duty on your purchase.

How it is calculated

Stamp duty = property value x stamp duty rate. The rate depends on the state and, in some states, on the buyer. Additional cess or surcharge, where applicable, is not included.

Example

On a ₹50,00,000 property in Delhi, a male buyer pays 6%, which is ₹3,00,000. A female buyer pays 4%, which is ₹2,00,000, saving ₹1,00,000.

How to use the Stamp Duty Calculator

  1. Fill in Property value (₹), State, Buyer, Custom stamp duty (%).
  2. Every field starts with an example value. Replace it with your own numbers or keep it to see how the tool works.
  3. Read the result panel. It updates instantly as you type, so there is no button to press.
  4. Change one value at a time to compare scenarios. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Quantities are theoretical estimates. Real projects need extra for wastage, site conditions and design changes.
  • Rates for materials, labour, stamp duty and registration vary by city and change often. Confirm with your contractor, engineer or the local registration office.

Frequently asked questions

Who pays stamp duty?

The buyer pays stamp duty when the sale deed is registered.

Are the state rates up to date?

They are indicative. States change rates and concessions often, so verify the current rate on the official state website.

Does stamp duty apply to gift or rent agreements?

Yes, but at different rates. This calculator is meant for property purchase.

Is this stamp duty calculator free to use?

Yes. The stamp duty calculator is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser, so the numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
